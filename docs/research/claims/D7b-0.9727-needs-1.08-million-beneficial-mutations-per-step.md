## Statement (verbatim)
> "The chance of a single step succeeding must be large – because we need to multiply it by itself 500 times (for the 500 steps) so that it comes out as close to 1/1,000,000 as possible (i.e. the chance of 1 in a million). The smallest number that will do this is 0.9727 as 1 – (1 – 1/300,000 ) 1,080,000 = 0.9727"

> "So if the odds that a specific nucleotide will mutate and take over a population are to be 0.9727 for each step, there must be 1,080,000 potential positive adaptive copying errors for each of the 500 steps to arrive at a 1 in a 1,000,000 chance for the development of a new species."

Source: [Every Critique is Correct](https://voxday.net/2024/04/25/every-critique-is-correct/), 2024-04-25, voxday.net, paras 6-8 (the formula is laid out across two lines in the extraction). `secondhand`.

## Formal statement
Let q = 1/300,000 be the per-candidate success probability and n the number of independent candidate beneficial mutations at each step. Step success P1 = 1 - (1 - q)^n. Required: P1^500 = 1e-6, i.e. P1 = 10^(-6/500) = 0.972747. Then n = ln(1 - P1)/ln(1 - q) = 1,080,778. Day: P1 = 0.9727 -> n = 1,080,259 -> '1,080,000'. Check: 1 - (1 - 1/300000)^1080000 = 0.972676 (Day prints 0.9727); 0.9727^500 = 9.76e-7 (~1e-6). Also n*q = 3.6 and 1 - e^-3.6 = 0.97268. All reconcile.

## Assumptions
- Stated: 'reasonable chance' = 1 in a million (stasis argument: species go for a very long time without changing); 500 steps; independence across steps; each candidate acts with probability 1/300,000.
- Implicit: the 1e-6 target is chosen by Spetner and is not derived; 'a step' undefined; 1.08 million candidates are the number of beneficial mutations at a single step, which Day says 'Nobody knows'.

## Responses
- Against: the requirement is a statement about biology (how many beneficial single-nucleotide options exist at a time) that the post does not test. For scale: 1.08e6 out of ~9.6e9 possible single-nucleotide changes in a 3.2 Gb genome (3 alternatives each) is 1.1e-4 (derived, not a Day figure); whether that many beneficial options exist at each step is the empirical question.
- In support: Day (post).
- Weaknesses: both the target (1e-6) and the model (500 sequential steps each requiring a success) are stipulations.
