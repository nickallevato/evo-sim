> "Rosenhouse’s book is objectively bad. It purports to be a critique of mathematics as used by ID proponents and of my mathematical work in particular. Yet it betrays a lack of comprehension throughout. It makes a virtue of misrepresentation."

Source: Bill Dembski, [Jason Rosenhouse's Whoppers: More Guidance on Reading Rosenhouse](https://billdembski.com/intelligent-design/jason-rosenhouses-whoppers/), 2022-08-26, section 3 'Appealing to the Unwashed Middle' (retrieved 2026-10-07 during R2; sha256 of saved HTML 42aed3b9d29993a5a232c07d19caca8f40cd8068cf4d010b172fbeecbd8a4236; not yet in bib-critics.md).

## Formal statement
No formal content. Two points: (1) the book is bad (assertion); (2) in the Weasel, the choice of the target-based fitness function is the information source (conservation-of-information argument; technical work by Dembski and colleagues, not retrieved).

## Assumptions
- Stated: Weasel's fitness function is chosen by the environment/designer; 'most fitness functions would evolve toward random sequences'.
- Implicit: the choice of fitness function in biology is analogous to choosing a target.
