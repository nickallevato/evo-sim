## Formal statement
No equation. Price/Robertson: selection response is Cov(w, z); a covariance is a descriptive statistic, not an event with a probability. The argument is about the wording of ROOT, not its arithmetic.

## Assumptions
- Stated: "selection" in Day's sentence means the covariance process.
- Implicit: Day's phrase "statistically impossible" means the covariance cannot exist, rather than that the observed divergence is far outside what the process can deliver in the time (his working claim, ROOT P3).

## Responses

- Weaknesses in the responses: a critic's rebuttal of the wording does not address the numbers; Hancock addresses the numbers elsewhere (B5c, G2, A3d).
