### Both sides' readings (each marked inference, nothing here changes a verdict)
**Day-side steelman.** (1) The data are public (T2T Primate Consortium; Progressive Cactus), so anyone can check the claim: FM10 meets the replication checklist better than most of his earlier posts. (2) Day hedges: the claim is "may", "suggests", "initial evidence", and he lists "far more outlandish" alternatives. (3) The overdispersion claim is testable and, if the variance-to-mean ratio is defined, falsifiable. (4) A lineage-asymmetric excess confined to contiguous blocks is a real pattern shape that uniform-rate neutral theory does not predict; the critics' own k = μ argument assumes a uniform mutation rate. (5) He concedes relictation is "not the whole story", i.e. he revises downward his own earlier mechanism.
**Critic-side steelman.** (1) Overdispersion of substitution density is the standard result of mutation-rate heterogeneity (CpG, replication timing, recombination-associated), alignment error in repeats and segmental duplications, and incomplete lineage sorting, none of which is selection or a bottleneck; "80 times more variable than a uniform-rate process" compares against a null no one uses. (2) Lineage assignment ("human-lineage") needs an outgroup; with ape-only genomes, ancestral polymorphism and ILS can place differences on the human branch. (3) Neutral theory predicts that fixation rate equals local mutation rate k = μ, so a high-μ mesa produces excess substitutions on *every* lineage; only a *human-specific* excess discriminates, and the post gives no per-lineage numbers or comparison to chimp- or gorilla-lineage mesas. (4) Comparing one mesa to a genome-wide Haldane maximum is a units step: Haldane's cost applies to selected substitutions, and the claim presupposes the mesa is selected rather than neutral. (5) FM8(b) "dominant source of genomic divergence" is stated without a fraction. (6) Neither selection nor drift "not viable" does not follow from a pattern both can contain; sigillation is introduced after the data, so it is not tested by it. Critic caution (pre-reg 5b): do not dismiss for the AI or UATV provenance; the data are public, so replicate.
**What would discriminate** (for the owner of checks): mesa coordinates and window parameters; the dispersion statistic; per-lineage counts for all ape branches; the same statistic on simulated neutral data with realistic μ heterogeneity; masking of repeats/SDRs and CpG; ILS-aware polarization.
