## Bottom line
1. **Human DFE (GAP-05 / B9 / HF): found.** Kim, Huber & Lohmueller 2017 (full text, OA) give the share of new nonsynonymous mutations by |s|; with the N = 10,000 rescaling they state, |s| from 1e-5 to 1e-3 is 2Ns of about 0.2 to 20. Boyko 2008 (already in bib; text re-pulled): 27-29% neutral or nearly neutral (|s| < 1e-4). Eyre-Walker, Woolfit & Phelps 2006 (abstract only): >50% mild (1e-3 to 1e-1), <15% strongly deleterious. Human DFE is **not** concentrated at Ns ~ 1: roughly a quarter to a third sits in |s| < 1e-4, about 15-17% in 1e-4 to 1e-3, and 50-60% above 1e-3 (model-dependent; the nearly neutral bins are the least accurate).
2. **Constraint:** Rands 2014: 8.2% (7.1-9.2%) of the human genome under negative selection (full text). Zoonomia 2023 (Science, abstract only; paywalled): about 10.7% (>= 332 Mb) constrained.
3. **Uricchio 2019:** full text via the PMC author manuscript. alpha = 0.135 (95% HPD 0.096-0.17), 72% from weakly beneficial alleles; VIPs 0.224 (0.17-0.28).
4. **CSAC 2005:** Ka/Ks = 0.23, 13,454 genes, found in the held PDF text. An independent genome-wide ML dN/dS number was **not found**: Nielsen 2005 (full text) and Bakewell 2007 (abstract only) report gene counts and tests, not a genome-wide mean. One raw ratio in Nielsen is flagged below as a trap, not a dN/dS.
5. **Good 2017 SI 5.2:** found. The HMM's fixed state F has no outgoing transition in the defining text or in Eq. (31); the SI never uses the word "absorbing". Details below.
6. **L2 hardness h:** Stearns 2010 is **Nat Rev Genet** 11:611 (not PNAS); Byars 2010 is PNAS 107(S1):1787. Both abstract-only (full texts blocked or paywalled). Neither abstract decomposes selection into viability and fertility; the Stearns abstract names lifetime reproductive success as the measure. Full-text check still open.

## 5. Good et al. 2017, Supplementary Information section 5.2
- Source: Springer SI link (`41586_2017_BFnature24287_MOESM1_ESM.pdf`), `good2017si/si.pdf`, sha256 `bb23faa5e97dd59cc6e3176d5720ee9c662ade6123ed6935f135d9cb762be9ba`; text via pdftotext. Section 5.2 "Simplest hidden Markov model", page 15 of the SI (Figure S4).
- Defining text: states A (ancestral), P (polymorphic), F (fixed), E (extinct). Prose: P can go to F or E; "In rare cases, mutations are allowed to transition from E back to A". Eq. (31): Pr[A to P] ~ 1e-2, Pr[P to F] ~ Pr[P to E] ~ 0.5, Pr[E to P] ~ 1e-6. No transition out of F is listed anywhere in the section. Figure S4 caption again shows only the E to A leak ("dashed line").
- Reading: F is absorbing by omission and design (the model is "an ad hoc Markov model", transition rates "fixed ... empirically", "relatively insensitive to the precise values"). The word "absorbing" does not occur in the SI text (0 matches). Internal slip: prose says E to A, Eq. (31) writes E to P. Day-side use: a fixed call cannot be revoked by the model, so a mutation called fixed stays fixed even if later reads show it polymorphic. Critic-side use: this is a state-labelling device with sequencing-error emissions (reads in F treated as errors); the authors say the results are insensitive to the rates, and validate with forward simulation (5.2.3, not read in detail here).
