**To be adapted for three kinds of reader:** (i) a population geneticist; (ii) a reader sympathetic to Vox Day's argument; (iii) a reader on the critics' side. The same brief goes to all three, so that nobody sees an easier task than another.

## What the project is
An open audit of the mathematics in the debate over Vox Day's *Probability Zero* and the replies to it. It is carried out by AI models (Claude) under one human owner; it has had no independent human expert review. It scores each claim on internal consistency, fidelity to sources, and fit to outside evidence, using one rule for all sides, and publishes the working. Read first: [`METHODS-AND-LIMITATIONS.md`](../METHODS-AND-LIMITATIONS.md) (about 15 minutes), then [`DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md).

## What we are asking you to look at
1. **Ten load-bearing nodes, re-derived blind (proposal f2 of [`METHOD-AUDIT-proposals.md`](../METHOD-AUDIT-proposals.md)).** You receive the verbatim quote and its source text, the three verdict definitions and the rule text with its worked examples removed. You do not receive our verdicts, write-ups or reviews. You say what the quote claims formally, compute it if you can, and give your own verdicts with reasons. Nodes: A2e (LTEE rate as a ceiling), B (neutral theory cannot rescue the shortfall), C2 (generation-overlap factor d), F1a (throughput versus latency in Day's reply), H5 (Hössjer, cost of parallel fixations), B3a (k = mu N/Ne), B7 (neutral fixation probability 1/(2N)), B4a (ancestral polymorphism in pairwise divergence), H8 (Kimura calculator, selection-cost term), A3 (required fixations, 30M then 20M). Pick as many as your time allows; a population geneticist is most useful on B3a, B7, B4a, H8, A2e.
2. **The verdict rule** ([`R4-X1-verdict-rule.md`](../../../research/checks/results/R4-X1-verdict-rule.md)): is the 25% materiality line, the reading-dependence rule and the rhetoric rule fair to both sides? Say where it bites one side harder.
3. **The limitations page:** what is missing, overstated or unfair?

## Time
About 2 hours for the rule and limitations page, plus 1 to 2 hours per node re-derived. Please tell us your own estimate if it differs; a partial review of three nodes is worth having.

## How disagreement is recorded
Your review is published with your name or a pseudonym as you choose. Where you disagree with a verdict, both are shown side by side in the claim file, with the date; a disagreement on a load-bearing node sends that node back to `pending` until a third reader, who sees both derivations and no names, adjudicates. The owner does not resolve disagreements between reviewers. A reviewer from either camp can write a dissent that is published unedited.

## Independence
You may stop at any time and withdraw your review before it is published. You are not asked to endorse the project or either author.
