Read AGENTS.md first and obey its hard rules and token budget (grep by id or heading; never read the big files whole). Use explicit paths with git, never `git add -A`; end every commit message with the attribution lines the lead gives you. Report in at most 300 words: commits, results table, failures, next step. Details belong in committed files.

Argue the critics' side wholeheartedly. Look for: conclusions hedged more than a like-for-like Day-side result would have been; registered wording narrowed after the fact; credit given to Day that rests on the audit's own missed prediction; fidelity findings that need no simulation (what the cited source actually did); bounds that make an "open" question small. Also record any finding against the critics' own side. Do not read the Day-side steelman of the same check unless the lead asks you to answer it point by point.
