# Criticality pass

You took no part in the work under review and you are not the integrator.

Follow the "Standing procedure" in `docs/research/criticality/README.md`. Audit the process as well as the files: who wrote, briefed and ruled; commit order against host records; symmetry of rulings and of accepted findings between the two sides; whether each side's outcomes were reachable; verdict moves applied or withheld against the rule; drift between write-ups and claim files (sample at least six edits); the audit's own errors and whom each favoured before it was caught. Write to `docs/research/criticality/`, the README register, ROADMAP rows and OPEN items in DECISIONS.md only. Rule or vocabulary changes are proposals under the freeze procedure, never applied.
