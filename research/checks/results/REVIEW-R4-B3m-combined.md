## 1. Correctness
Hand checks from the raw output: 0.043/0.00213 = 20.2 (reported 20.2); 0.886/0.043 = 20.6 vs PH1 median 21.4 (median of ratios, consistent); B1/B4 identical as expected (mu_gen_mod = mu_yr_mod x gen); B5 5.8-6.0 matches the table; 46 + 9 domestic = 55 consistent. Scoring against the docstring: D1 hit, A1 hit, A2 hit, A3 hit (20x >= 10x), C1 miss, A4 miss. All six scores follow the pre-registered thresholds as written.
- **MAJOR-1.** A5 ("record what scale makes the median ratio 1") was promised in the docstring and the raw output only says "see table"; the write-up never states the rescaling factor. Without it the claim "Bergeron draw equality lines" cannot be tied to the 25. Report the factor or move A5 to Open.
- **MAJOR-2.** The headline of the "Reading" (external contested, "leaning against falsified") rests on PH2 (k/mu about 1.9). That figure is root-to-tip over one root age, and root-to-tip saturates, so it is a lower bound on a lineage-average k, not an estimate. The write-up says "crude" but still uses it to relabel the 25 as an estimator choice. Wording should say: a lineage-average k is at least about 2x mu; the gap between 2 and 25 is unexplained, not shown to be an artefact.
- **MINOR-1.** Post hoc labelling: the "crude per-year k / median mu = 1.9" line is already in the pre-registered main run (U line), so 1.9 is not wholly post hoc; PH2 only refines it to a per-species median (1.85). The write-up table lists 1.85 as post hoc without noting the main-run 1.9. Add one clause.
- **MINOR-2.** PH1 uses 67 of 68 names matched and a tree of 74 tips; PH2 n = 55 via the same matching. Which species failed to match is not stated.
- **MINOR-3.** Reptile PH2 median 0.49 rests on n = 2; the group split should not be quoted.
- **MINOR-4.** Disclosure of the red-panda row is present and its k/mu ~22 sits inside the D1 and A1 ranges, so the informed predictions are soft; the write-up should say A1 and A4's non-trivial content is the direction and the count, not the magnitude.
## 2. Day-side steelman
- Day names Bergeron 2023, 55 vertebrates, median 25; all three reproduce (25.4, n = 55, matched per-year and per-generation). A4 missed in his favour. This is a clean fidelity `accurate`, and the write-up credits it.
- **MINOR-5.** Rule C asks for the most charitable reading of "phylogenetic k". Terminal-branch chronos rates are what Bergeron tabulate as the species substitution rate, so Day's reading is the plain one, and the write-up says so. But the "Reading" bullet then discounts the terminal-rate k in favour of a lineage-average k that Day never used; a Day-side reader will say the audit swapped the estimator. Phrase it as "the 25 is a property of the tabulated rate, and the tabulated rate is not the lineage-average rate", not as a rebuttal of Day.
- The direction result (k > mu) fits a k = mu failure in Day's direction; nothing in B3m supports the purifying-selection reading. Recorded prominently, good.
- Terminal-edge rates at lambda = 0 may be inflated by short durations; but they are also the rates the authors publish as their data. A residual ~2x on the lineage basis is not zero.
## 3. Critic-side steelman
- Strongest critic point is PH1: the column implies a 21 Myr depth against 416 Myr, so the column is not a lineage-average rate, and k = mu is a statement about long-run averages. Supported by the numbers, with MAJOR-2's caveat.
- **MINOR-6.** Critics would add that mu is a per-generation germline rate while terminal k includes polymorphism and recent-branch segregating variants (not-yet-fixed), which inflate short-branch k. The write-up lists "short terminal durations" but not this; add it as a candidate cause.
- **MINOR-7.** The critic steelman C1 was wrong in direction (miss); the write-up credits this honestly. Note that the critics' broader claim (no robust 25-fold gap) is still supported by PH2 only as a lower bound.
## Verdict
Fidelity `accurate` stands; external `contested` stands but the "leaning against falsified" phrase should be softened per MAJOR-2. Fix MAJOR-1 and MAJOR-2 wording before integration; no rerun needed (R + chronos lambda > 0 stays in Open).
