Reviewer role: argue as the strongest honest critic (Hancock, Nesslig20, keruru, McCarthy, Fun-Friendship4898) would. **What the critics get right here (should lead the section):**
1. 32.3 exists only when SDR base pairs are divided by a point-mutation rate. With events it is 2.25-3.38. With base pairs counted like-for-like it is about 0.4-0.6.
2. After the ancestral term at the R1 value, the residual is about 1 at human mu, and about 1 at Langergraber's split time. It is not an order of magnitude.
3. The input that Day attributes to Yoo is not in Yoo (A3x1).
