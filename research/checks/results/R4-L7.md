## Interpretation
- **Derivation of the unstated.** The reading that best matches Day's new wording ("three different tools on three different bases"; sheets 14, 19, 24) is R1: Table III.14's mean SNV plus mean gap divergence (13.94%), applied to hs1's length from Table III.19. That gives 404-413 M, and it puts 410 M on a sourced footing in Yoo's SI that A3x1 had marked `not-found`.
  - The match does not prove the route. Three routes land within 2%, and the base rate says such matches are common.
  - The 14.9% is not reproduced exactly on any hs1 denominator, but 1 - identical-1:1 = 15.05% sits 0.15 points from it, within a rounding or segment-choice difference. That reading gives 436.5 M, not 410 M.
  - **Tension with Day's own wording (critic-side, MINOR-7).** R1 adds SNV and gap divergence. Day's new post says the numbers "aren't additive". If they are not additive, his 410 M cannot be the sum R1 forms; if R1 is his route, they are additive. The post does not say which.
  - **Day's printed components (Zenodo 23003785 s7.1, MAJOR-1).** "approximately 35 million SNVs, 1,140 interspecific inversions, and approximately 187 megabases of structurally divergent regions, for a total of approximately 410 million genomic differences". Literally summed, 35 M + 1,140 + 187 M = **222.0 M**, 0.54 of 410 M. This was scored on A3a under R4-X1 (rule C: 187 Mb read as SDR per lineage, 35 M + 1,140 + 2 x 187 M = 409.0 M, ledger slip, internal `holds`). That charitable reading is an R3-type sum; on Yoo's own sheet 24 it gives 330-402 M depending on the haplotype. None of R1, R2 or the 2 x 187 reading is the list Day printed.
- **Unit.** All reproducing readings are base pairs. Gap divergence counts bases in gaps, so one 1 Mb indel contributes 1 Mb. "410 million differences, not 35 million" therefore compares a base-pair total with a CSAC event count (A3x).
- **The CSAC framing.** "The insertions and deletions ... were quietly excluded" is a fidelity question for the 2005 paper. CSAC's abstract-level text the repo holds reports indel events ("~5 million compared with ~35 million"; A3x), so indels were reported, as a count. The audit has not checked the 98.77% / denominator wording against the paper.

## Who this helps
- **Day.** Strongest defence (review MINOR-5): a "genomic difference" can mean a base that fails to align or match, which is what R1 counts, so "410 M vs 35 M" contrasts amounts of differing sequence. His new locator is accurate: sheets 14, 19 and 24 hold the relevant divergence measures, they are on different bases, and no consolidated total is given. A base-pair total near 410 M follows from sheets 14 x 19. The A3x1 "not-found" label should be revised to "reconstructible (R1), route unconfirmed".
- **Critics.** The reconstructed 410 M is a base-pair measure, it needs the mean gap divergence (the median gives 82 M), it adds pieces Day now calls non-additive, and his own printed components sum to 222 M. Set against events (48 M for both lineages on T2T) it is 8.5x. The "not 35 million" contrast mixes units, and the bp-versus-events point (A3x) stands.

## Caveats for the reviewer
1. Mean gap divergence per 1 Mb segment, multiplied by total length, is the audit's reconstruction. The SI does not multiply.
2. Sheet 14 pairs both chimp haplotypes with both HG002 haplotypes. hs1 (CHM13) lengths are used for the multiplication.
