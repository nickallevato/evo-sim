## Interpretation
- **Derivation of the unstated.** The reading that best matches Day's new wording ("three different tools on three different bases"; sheets 14, 19, 24) is R1: Table III.14's mean SNV plus mean gap divergence (13.94%), applied to hs1's length from Table III.19. That gives 404-413 M, and it puts 410 M on a sourced footing in Yoo's SI that A3x1 had marked `not-found`.
  - The match does not prove the route. Three routes land within 2%, and the base rate says such matches are common.
  - The 14.9% is not reproduced on any hs1 denominator. The closest is 1 - identical-1:1 = 15.05%.
- **Unit.** All reproducing readings are base pairs. Gap divergence counts bases in gaps, so one 1 Mb indel contributes 1 Mb. "410 million differences, not 35 million" therefore compares a base-pair total with a CSAC event count (A3x).
- **The CSAC framing.** "The insertions and deletions ... were quietly excluded" is a fidelity question for the 2005 paper. CSAC's abstract-level text the repo holds reports indel events ("~5 million compared with ~35 million"; A3x), so indels were reported, as a count. The audit has not checked the 98.77% / denominator wording against the paper.

## Who this helps
- **Day.** His new locator is accurate: sheets 14, 19 and 24 hold the relevant divergence measures, they are on different bases, and no consolidated total is given. A base-pair total near 410 M follows from sheets 14 x 19. The A3x1 "not-found" label should be revised to "reconstructible (R1), route unconfirmed".
- **Critics.** The reconstructed 410 M is a base-pair measure. Set against events (48 M for both lineages on T2T) it is 8.5x. The "not 35 million" contrast mixes units, and the bp-versus-events point (A3x) stands.
