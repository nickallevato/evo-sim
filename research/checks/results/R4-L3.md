## 6. Proposed effect on verdicts (proposals only; integration decides)

were adaptive and does not split them by effect size. So L3 speaks to the **any-effect-size per-generation count
reading**, the reading of A2e's formal statement. That is the reading the superseded `contradicted` value was
scoped to. Per the docstring, L3 licenses **no move** on the strong-effect-only or fitness-weighted readings,
in any band. It licenses no direct move on A or F2 and has no bearing on RG-03 (it is coding-only). A2e internal
(`non-sequitur`) and fidelity are untouched.

## 7. Post hoc, descriptive observations (no band, no verdict)

1. **The correction is large and the corrected value is about zero.** The classic MK over all bins is -0.8423
   (bootstrap [-0.8836, -0.8030]). The window-only value is -0.0789 ([-0.1155, -0.0444]). The asymptote is
   -0.0200. Most of the correction comes from the low-frequency bins (bin 1 alpha -0.996). Above x ≈ 0.3 the
   binned alpha(x) scatters around zero (section 2) and shows no rise toward x = 1.
2. **Uricchio's aMK baseline is not reproduced as a point, but it is not excluded.** The docstring describes
   Uricchio's 0.076 as computed on "the same 661 African samples and the same hg19/panTro4/ponAbe2 parsimony
   divergence". L3's point is 0.096 below it. Yet 0.076 lies inside both of L3's intervals (bootstrap upper
   0.0921; MC upper 48.9101).
3. **The interval reaches far above the flip.** The bootstrap upper bound 0.0921 is 5.3x h. Uricchio's ABC-MK
   lower HPD (0.096, the superseded basis) sits just above the bootstrap upper bound and inside the MC interval.
   L3 neither confirms nor excludes it.
4. **Demography contrast (S5, EUR).** The EUR point is 0.0175 below AFR (Q6 hit). The EUR classic MK is more
   negative (-1.0062 vs -0.8423), as a bottleneck would make it. The asymptote moves much less than the classic
   value does.
5. **Width of the bootstrap interval alone** is 0.1664. That is also over Q5's 0.15, so Q5 would miss even
   without the MC upper tail.

## 8. Who this helps

**Day side.**
- The audit's own bias-corrected human-lineage alpha, on public data with a pre-registered pipeline, comes out
  at or below zero: P = -0.0200, every S1-S5 point below l, all points below h. On this estimator the human
  lineage shows no adaptive excess over the flip at ~1%. DS and DS+ hit.
- Under the registered rule, the "aMK baseline 0.076, 4.4x" line in the `contradicted` basis would be struck as
  not reproduced. A second-order Day point (Day's position as the brief summarises it, not a scored quote): two
  aMK estimates on what the registration calls the same samples differ by 0.096, which is wider than the whole
  alpha* band.

**Critic side.**
- L3 does not put the interval below the flip. U ≥ l under either interval, and the bootstrap upper bound is
  5.3x h. No band below B5 was reached, and the registration stated in advance that even B6 would stop at a
  lean, because aMK "may still have a tendency toward underestimation" (HM7).
- The correction from the classic MK (Q3, 0.8224) is very large. This bears out the critics' general point,
  and the audit's own reason for setting aside Gayà-Vidal's uncorrected 0.2%: naive human MK values are heavily
  biased low.
- Uricchio's 0.076 and the 0.096 lower HPD are not excluded by L3's intervals (section 7, items 2-3).
- CS and CS+ missed. The critic side gets no confirmation from L3.

**Both / the audit's own errors.**

   (l.469), so these pass. The band does not hinge on it (section 2); Q5 does.
3. **AA case convention not quoted.** See section 8, "Both". The docstring makes the quote a precondition for
   the write-up using the reading.
4. **Negative and impossible counts.** The P and L count rows are negative, and the U row exceeds Dn (section 5).
   They are printed as registered.
5. **"Not reproduced on the same samples."** The registered strike rule applies in B4-B6. Here 0.076 lies inside
   L3's interval, and the "same samples" description of Uricchio's pipeline is the docstring's. Gene set,
   binning, window and fit may differ. Whether the strike follows as worded is for the reviews.
