## 2. Day-side steelman
- **MINOR-4 (the comparison favours Day more than the write-up lets on).** The +14.7% and 24.14 M put Day's "~20 million fixations" in the low end; the write-up says so. But also: the pre-registered critic band (17.9-24.2 M) was set at GAP-07b's 21.05 +/-15%, and the result sits at its very edge. On T2T the critic-side reading of "events not bp" holds while the quantitative centre has moved toward Day. The write-up's "Both: nothing moves" is true under R1 but Day can fairly add that two successive assembly upgrades (hg38 to T2T) both pushed counts up, and that unaligned sequence (80.3 M hs1 bp with no chimp row; 242.3 M chimp bp with no human row in the chimp pass) is excluded from events, so 24.14 M is a floor on what whole-genome comparison implies, not a ceiling.
- **NIT-4.** The ratio 8.49 is nearer to Day's side than 9.74; it still leaves 205 M as 8.5x the event count. The write-up notes both.

## 3. Critic-side steelman
- **MINOR-5 (205 M versus the critic band).** The critic reading, "the 205 M bp is a base-pair count, an event count is ~20 M", is stated as 205 / events = 8.5-11.5 (C1). The result 8.49 sits just outside, and the F1 (10.38), polymorphism-corrected (9.2-10.9) and "single-copy" bases fall inside. The honest headline for critics is: the ratio ranges 8.5 (F0 raw) to 10.9 (polymorphism-corrected 0.78), with F0 raw the least critic-favourable end. The write-up does present these, but its Interpretation states "8.5x ... (10.4x in single-copy blocks)" without the polymorphism-corrected 9.2-10.9. Add it so the critic-favourable end appears next to the Day-favourable one.
- **NIT-5.** F0 counts paralogous first-row alignments (stated in caveats), and gorilla-only polarisation leaves 2.49 M SNVs unpolarised and 0.56 M "third"; both are noise relative to the 14.7% move but both lean toward overcounting events in duplicated blocks, which is the critic point about unreliable alignment in T2T-added regions. The Y chromosome (0.62 M per lineage; ampliconic) is where that is sharpest and the write-up mentions it.
- **NIT-6.** The chimp-side polymorphism correction assumes the human share, because no chimp VCF was found. If chimp diversity is higher (as is generally reported), the corrected event count is lower and the ratio higher (critic-favourable). The write-up marks this as the open part; fine.
