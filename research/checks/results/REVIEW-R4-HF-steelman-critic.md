# Review of R4-HF (steelman, critics' side)

Reviewer role: argue as the most capable and honest critic of Day would. This critic stands in the Wallace (soft selection), Nunney 2003, Kimura-Crow / Maynard Smith (truncation) and Lesecque-Keightley-Eyre-Walker (relative fitness, synergy) line. Questions asked: does HF model the critics' mechanisms as they propose them, and does the write-up frame the results fairly to them?

Reviewed (2026-10-11):
- `research/checks/results/R4-HF.md` (adbff0a);
- the pre-registration docstring of `hf_followups.py` (8bd9d29) and `run()`;
- `raw/hf_main.jsonl`, `hf_analyse.out`, `hf_ci.out`, `hf_gap05.out` (local recomputation: per-rep k/lam, nopen, N/K, La in the mix and synergy cells);
- `sources/prior-art.md` PA-09 (Wallace), PA-14 (Nunney 2003), PA-15 (Crow & Kimura 1979), PA-16 (Lesecque et al. 2012), PA-17; `ledgers/gaps.md` GAP-05; `results/R4-H-C2.md`;
- `REVIEW-R4-H8-steelman-critic.md`; R4-X1.

Overall: CA1, CB1 and PB1 are robust and fairly scored. Soft selection and truncation sustain two orders of magnitude more than Haldane's rate in-model, and the realised rate stays far above 1/300 even in cells the rule calls failures. But three choices tilt the remaining results toward Day:
- the steady-state rule's small-number noise depresses the partly-hard results;
- the synergy arm penalises the number of concurrent sweeps instead of modelling synergy's variance mechanism;
- HF-d's S3 is the upper-end scenario of every input at once, and it is read as "real".

## Verdict support (critic-side view)
- **H:** external contested. In-model the cap disappears under relative fitness and noise-free truncation (realised rates ≥ 200x 1/300). It is within 1-3x of the hard cap at h = 0.3 and ≈ 3-5.5x at h = 0.1. Which regime humans are in is unsourced on both sides.
- **H8:** s3.3.1 holds of s_max. Term 3 as a rate bound is contradicted in-model for soft selection and truncation, and supported for hard and h ≥ 0.3. Synergy as modelled here is not a test of the critics' mechanism. External: contested.
- **H5:** conditional on h and R.
- **Drift Deathmarch:**
  - The crux ("You cannot turn selection off for neutral mutations while keeping it on for harmful ones") is contradicted by per-mutation fixation (S1).
  - The 9-generation collapse needs the premise no critic holds.
- **HO-11:** "declines over time" only at the stacked upper end; "quite rapidly dies" is contradicted (≈ 1e-5 per generation).
