- Table sums reproduce: strict 5,496, within-lineage 7,166, first crossing 8,679, inflation 3,183, 723,000 generations. The naive rule overcounts the strict one by 57.9%; 36.7% of the naive count is removed.
- G_f moves from 1,322 (snapshot) to 1,587 under the strict rule: **+20.1%**. The naive rule gives 824 (-37.7% against 1,322). Spread across rules (a)-(d): 3.04x (824 to 1,587 is 1.9x among (a)-(c)).

- The snapshot series by timepoint reproduces (12.6, 21.4, 33.4, 29.6, 36.8, 45.4); Ara+5 reads 16, 13, 38, 34, 14, 0, and its last sampled generation is 57,500, so its 60K cell lies beyond its last sample.
- Mutators (7): strict 5,307 over 424,500 generations, 80 gen/fix; first crossing 8,315, 51 gen/fix. Strict Ara-2 is 94, not negative.

## What this does and does not settle
- Settles: the arithmetic of every figure quoted in E5 and E6, and the rule-dependence of G_f (824 / 1,322 / 1,587, plus 523 if within-lineage sweeps are counted).
- Does not settle: whether the strict state calls are right (E6 fidelity stays unverifiable; no Good 2017 files); whether "-906" is an estimator artefact (formula not in text; E5 external stays pending). What it adds: three other rules do not give Ara+5 zero (14, 63, 119) and none gives Ara-2 a negative count, so the zero and negative cells are specific to the snapshot/clone-pair constructions, which is what the critics said, and the later Day paper concedes by abandoning them.

## Who this helps
- **Critics (Dumb-and-Dumber, Sparky_6_4):** their two quotes are accurate to the paper, and the rule comparison shows the Ara+5 drop to 0 is not reproduced by any other rule from the same publication. The naive 95% rule gives G_f 824, 38% faster than 1,322.
- **Day:** he published the correction himself; the strict rule (1,587) is 20% slower than the snapshot figure, so it moves the LTEE rate in the direction of his larger shortfall. His abstract's statement that counts are rule-dependent (a 2-3x range) is borne out (3.04x here). The strict count does not require the clone-pair method the critics attacked.
- **Both:** G_f is a rule-dependent number (824 to 1,587), so any conclusion that holds only at one value inherits that sensitivity.
