# Pre-run design review of R4 FMnull (neutral-null calibration of Day's overdispersion index)
Date 2026-10-12. Status of the check: pre-registered 48241c7, pre-run amendment cc5e6dd (SOURCED parameter set beside PREREG), **not run** beyond `selftest`. This is a design review made before any result exists; it does not replace the three post-run reviews (load-bearing tier). Method: read the docstring and code of `fm_mesa_null.py`, `refresh-2026-10-12c-lit.md`, the FML-* quotes and the CSAC2005 quotes, the QUEUE entry, the FM paragraph of RESULTS.md and the "Who this helps" line of R4-FM.md. Hand arithmetic only; nothing was run; `raw/fm_analyse.json` was not opened. The script was not edited. Same-agent-family review (independence caveat). Every amendment below would be labelled **post hoc (pre-run, no data seen)**. Items marked "memory" are not from held text and must be verified before use.

Arithmetic re-derived and confirmed: SOURCED M1 lo corner 0.04 x g(2) = 0.0227, others 0.0005, CV 0.152; hi corner 0.0625 x g(0.5) = 0.0533, others 0.0198, CV 0.270; N_lo at 1 Mb about 286 and at 100 kb about 48 (U-DHC, c = 1); two-epoch ILS E[T2] = 2.75 My, sd 2.41 My, sigma 0.352; BGS bracket [0.0077, 0.149]; D_struct algebra for U-PAIRS (naive and structure-aware).

## 1. Correctness
- **MAJOR-1 (the SOURCED M1 bracket is a constraint at 1 Mb, not a rectangle).** Smith's number fixes the CV of the 1-Mb window mean, i.e. sigma^2 x g(1 Mb / ell), not sigma. The script calibrates sigma at one corner per edge (sigma_lo with ell_lo, sigma_hi with ell_hi) and then reads the band at every W from those two corners. The docstring's monotonicity argument ("the band is the bracket image") no longer holds, because sigma and ell are now tied. Holding the 1-Mb CV fixed and moving ell across its bracket: at 100 kb the M1 term at the lo edge is 0.0375 at the registered corner but 0.0262 at (sigma 0.163, ell 2 Mb), and at the hi edge 0.0615 at the registered corner but 0.0879 at (sigma 0.306, ell 0.5 Mb). So for W < 1 Mb the SOURCED band is too narrow on both sides: M1's part of N_lo is overstated by about 1.4x and its part of N_hi understated by about 1.4x (N_hi masked at 100 kb about 163 -> about 195, hand). For W >= 1 Mb the registered corners are the extremes and the band is right. Direction: the understated N_hi makes DAY easier to reach at W < 1 Mb; the overstated N_lo makes BELOW more likely. Fix: amendment 1.
- **MAJOR-2 (lo edge 0.05 -> 0.20: defensible only as a present-day DNM floor).** The conversion itself is done correctly: the comparable model quantity is the CV of the window mean over M0-M3 + CPG, and the arithmetic checks. What is not established is that the lowest of three pedigree datasets is a floor for the rate variance relevant to six million years of divergence. Smith's caveat (differences "probably as a consequence of differences in sequencing technology and processing") says the spread between datasets is not a biological range; it does not say which dataset is right, and technical variance can inflate all three. FML-S8 (divergence tracks DNM density "only about half as strong as it could") has two readings: extra variance sources in divergence (lo edge fine) or a mutation landscape that is not stable over time (lo edge too high; if only half persists, sigma_M1 is about 0.10 and N_lo at 1 Mb is about 70, not 280). The choice changes a registered cell: P1 at 1 Mb moves from "inside" to BELOW. This is reading-dependent and should be reported as such, not settled by picking 0.20. Fix: amendment 2.
- **MAJOR-3 (Smith's slopes are applied downward only).** The amendment uses FML-S5 to derive 100-kb CVs and checks M2 against them, and flags 10 kb. The same slopes applied upward, inside Smith's fitted range (10 kb - 10 Mb), give 10-Mb CVs of 0.101 (Francioli), 0.214 (Wong), 0.104 (Jonsson). The SOURCED model gives 0.064 (lo) to 0.172 (hi) at 10 Mb: the lo edge is 39% below the lowest derived value and the hi edge 20% below Wong. An exponential kernel with ell <= 2 Mb falls as W^-0.5 beyond its scale, steeper than the quoted slopes (-0.10 to -0.25); M0 is unsourced and too small to make up the difference. Affects every cell at W >= 3 Mb and the R2 crossing in P3. Derived, not quoted. Fix: amendment 3.
- **MAJOR-4 (the frozen scoring rule is not what `score` implements).** The docstring lists exposure and d among the inputs; `score(W, value, reading, unit, masked)` takes neither. N_hi always uses c = 1.0, including for masked data, where exposure is below 1 by construction; D - 1 is proportional to c, so N_hi can be overstated by 2x or more, most of the 3x margin. `inhouse` (P4, P5) likewise uses the c bracket [0.5, 1] for every cell instead of the cell's own mean exposure, which widens each band by 2x and makes "P5 >= 80% inside" easier to pass. Fix: amendment 4.
- **MAJOR-5 (P4 and P5 are informed, not predictive).** R4-FM's phi values were committed before this registration; RESULTS.md states the range ("3x to 800x") and R4-FM.md that the index is "above 80x at 1 Mb against a uniform rate, for every lineage". The unsourced PREREG brackets were written with that knowledge available, whether or not the JSON was opened. P4 ("the window at which the data reach 80 lies inside the band") and P5 are consistency checks against known numbers and should be scored as such (the L7 MAJOR-2 precedent). The lookup table and the `score` mode are unaffected.
- **MINOR-1 (ILS derivation).** Delta = 2.25 My is **inferred**, not quoted: it solves exp(-Delta / 3.25 My) = 0.5 from FML-H2 ("around 50%") with N_HC from FML-H1. The algebra is right, and there is a held cross-check: Scally's "In 30% of the genome, gorilla is closer to human or chimpanzee" implies 2/3 x P(deep) = 0.30, P(deep) = 0.45, consistent with "around 50%". Hobolth's own estimate of the second speciation time should be quoted from the held text in place of the inference.
- **MINOR-2 (ILS and window size).** The per-locus sigma is a tract-scale quantity and is correctly independent of W; the model does average it down, through g(W / ell), which is what makes its contribution to D the W-independent 2 d sigma^2 ell. That part is right. The kernel shape is not: for two lineages in one population the correlation of coalescence times at scaled distance rho is (rho + 18) / (rho^2 + 13 rho + 18) (memory: Griffiths 1981 / Hudson 1983; verify), which has a 1/rho tail, so the window-mean variance falls as log(W)/W and the ILS term in D grows slowly with log W. With N 65,000 and r = 1e-8 the equivalent exponential ell is about 1.1 kb at W = 10 kb, 2.1 kb at 100 kb and 3.0 kb at 1 Mb. So ell_hi = 2 kb is roughly central at large W, not an upper edge, and ell_lo = 100 bp is 10-30x low. Size: the ILS term is about 7-10 against a hi-edge value of 9.3, a few percent of N_hi at W >= 100 kb. No outcome moves; correct the "W-independent" wording for ILS.
- **MINOR-3 (BGS formula).** The fix (mean fraction times sd(B)/mean(B)) is right to first order. In the two-epoch model the elasticity of E[T2] to B is 0.87, not 1 (derived), a 13% overstatement. The hi edge uses 0.44, the single-epoch central value, while the text gives the two-epoch range as 0.30-0.46. sd(B) is still unsourced and spans 5x, which dominates.
- **MINOR-4 (double counting).** SOURCED: none found between BGS and the mutation terms. M1 is calibrated on pedigree DNMs, which carry no BGS, ILS or gBGC signal, and CPG and M3 are inside the sum that is matched to Smith, so they are not added twice. PREREG cannot be audited on this point: its brackets are unsourced, and if they echo divergence-based estimates of "regional mutation rate" they already contain BGS, ILS and gBGC. Residual overlaps to state: M0 ("subtelomeric") against GBGC (FML-D1 ties the telomere gradient to recombination); technical variance inside the DNM CV.
- **MINOR-5.** P7's "corr(H density, gene-density covariate) < 0 under BGS" is true by construction of the simulation (the field is multiplied by 1 - s z); not a test. The BGS arm uses ell = 1 Mb, not the bracket edge. TREE branch lengths are unsourced although Yoo 2025 SI III.14 is held; the (H,C,B,G) stem looks short against the orangutan side. The same ILS sigma is applied to G, TREE and PAIRS (disclosed). `window_var` at 100-bp bins overstates components with ell = 100-200 bp by about 8%.
- Checked and fine: `expected_sample_D` finite-genome correction; `classify` thresholds; `tree_struct`; R1x as D - 1.

## 2. Day-side steelman
- **MAJOR-6 (the scoring rule is not symmetric).** Day scores only above 3x N_hi, and N_hi is the simultaneous upper corner: all seven components at sigma_hi and ell_hi, d at its upper value, exposure 1. The critic side scores anywhere inside [N_lo, N_hi] with no margin, including the top of the band, which is reachable only at that corner. No central value is reported. Under PREREG the band is 23x wide at 100 kb (5.7 to 133), so a value of 120 scores CRITIC, "unremarkable", though it needs every component near its maximum. Fix: amendment 5.
- **MAJOR-7 (the unmasked upper band rests on an assumed artefact).** N_hi(unmasked) adds ALN with sigma up to 0.42, unsourced, which roughly doubles the band at 100 kb (hand: 133 -> 288 PREREG, 163 -> 319 SOURCED). Alignment error is not a neutral evolutionary process. A value that is inside only because of ALN_hi would be scored "unremarkable for neutral heterogeneity"; it should be labelled as depending on an artefact term of unknown size.
- **MINOR-6 (BELOW).** Under SOURCED an index of 80 at 1 Mb is BELOW, "no verdict move". For Day this is not nothing: if real indices sit under the neutral lower band, the model over-predicts and the instrument that scores CRITIC elsewhere is miscalibrated there. Record BELOW as a bracket failure against the null, visibly (see also critic MINOR-8: the same outcome is a fortiori "not anomalously high").
- **MINOR-7 (what the null is).** Q171 says neither selection nor neutral drift. The literature the amendment cites attributes the variance in human-chimp divergence largely to selection in the ancestor (FML-M3, FML-P1). Day can use that against "neutral drift suffices"; the band that answers his full sentence is K_hi (known mechanisms including BGS), not N_hi. Score against both.
- **MINOR-8.** S3 asks for edges "sharper than the shortest-ell component allows"; the shortest ell is 100-200 bp, below the 1-kb bin, so the condition cannot be met or failed on the held data. And Smith's finding that covariates explain under 40% of the explainable variance makes weak covariate correlation the neutral norm, which weakens S3 as a Day-side discriminator and equally weakens "covariate correlations" as a critic-side one.
- Credit in the design: P3 records R2 as a Day-side point if it holds; P8 credits the limit of the critics' main test; cells above the band are to be reported, not hidden.

## 3. Critic-side steelman
- **MAJOR-8 (a DAY outcome is close to guaranteed by reading R2, without reproduction).** `score` classifies Day's stated number against the band. Under R2 (D > 6400) P3 already predicts "above N_hi(unmasked) at every W <= 1 Mb" for H, C, G and DHC, and the verdict rule moves FM3 and FM10 on "DAY on the most charitable plausible reading". But rule RD's charitable reading is the one under which the statement is true, and R4-FM's own indices do not reach 6400 below 1 Mb (the docstring says so in P4). A number the data do not reproduce is not an anomaly of the genome. Fix: amendment 6.
- **MAJOR-9 (two plausible readings of the statistic are missing).** No method is released, so these are as admissible as R2. (a) Counts per fixed genomic window, not normalised for alignable bases: exposure then varies between windows and a uniform rate gives D - 1 = m x CV_c^2; at 100 kb (m about 1,370 for DHC) a CV of exposure of 0.2-0.5 gives 55-340, enough by itself to reach 80. The model treats exposure as a constant. (b) "Substitution density" counted in base pairs including gaps, which is how Day counts divergence elsewhere (the 410 M figure, R4-L7 / A3x): the count is then compound, and a uniform-rate process gives D = E[L^2] / E[L] over event sizes, which is large for a heavy-tailed indel length distribution. The U-PAIRS-struct arm already has the algebra. Fix: amendment 7.
- **MAJOR-10 (the neutral bound for lineage specificity is too low).** S2 scores DAY when phi_HC exceeds 3x its neutral upper bound, and that bound contains only the lineage-specific share of gBGC (plus ALN). Every mutational component is taken as fully shared by human and chimp for six million years. FML-S8 says the divergence-DNM correlation is about half its maximum "and this correlation gets worse as more divergent species are considered", which is direct evidence that part of the landscape is not shared; assembly-specific error is also lineage-specific. Since S2 is the criterion that would separate a fourth mechanism from known ones, the tilt here is toward Day. Fix: amendment 8.
- **MINOR-9 (BELOW, critic side).** For the question FM3 poses ("is 80 anomalously high?"), a value under the neutral lower band is a fortiori not anomalous. Treating it as "no verdict move" withholds a critic-side score that PREREG would give at the same W. With Day MINOR-6, the outcome needs two labels, one per question.
- **MINOR-10.** Fixing MAJOR-1 raises N_hi at W < 1 Mb by about 19% at 100 kb; as registered the SOURCED set is slightly easier on Day there.
- **MINOR-11.** The asymmetry "CRITIC on every plausible reading, DAY on the most charitable one" follows rule RD (Day is the speaker) and is acceptable once MAJOR-8 is fixed; say so in the write-up.

## Are the readings, units and rules fair? (summary)
Readings R1, R1x and R2 are fair to carry; R2 needs the reproduction gate; two readings are missing (MAJOR-9). Units H, C, G, DHC, TREE and PAIRS cover "across the genome" and "all six ape genomes"; the base-pair unit is missing and TREE is unsourced. The DAY and CRITIC rules are not symmetric, in three places that push in different directions: the 3x margin exists only on the upper side (toward critics), BELOW is unscored (toward Day), and the lineage-specific bound is too low (toward Day). The net tilt cannot be stated pre-run; each should be fixed separately.

## Recommended pre-run amendments
All would be labelled post hoc (pre-run, no data seen). PREREG stays as registered; changes apply to SOURCED or add outputs.
1. **M1 as a constraint envelope.** For each ell in the bracket solve sigma(ell) to match the 1-Mb CV (0.15 lo, 0.27 hi) and take the minimum and maximum of V(W) over ell at each W. *Raises N_hi and lowers N_lo at W < 1 Mb: helps critics at the top, removes spurious BELOW at the bottom.*
2. **Lo edge as reading-dependent.** Report N_lo for sigma_M1 lo = 0.05 (PREREG), 0.10 (half of the DNM variance persists, FML-S8) and 0.20 (DNM floor), side by side, and let no outcome rest on one of them. *Both sides; removes a choice the sources do not settle.*
3. **Use Smith's slopes at 10 Mb too.** Either a SOURCED M0 calibrated to the derived 10-Mb CV (0.10-0.21), or flag every cell at W >= 3 Mb as under-sourced. *Raises the band at large W: helps critics under R2; label as derived.*
4. **Implement the frozen inputs.** `score` takes exposure and d; P4 and P5 use each cell's own exposure. Report the bracket version beside it. *Lowers N_hi for masked data: helps Day; tightens P5: neutral.*
5. **Central value and two labels.** Print N_c (geometric centre) beside N_lo and N_hi; label values inside the band but above 3 x N_c as "upper-corner only", and values inside only with ALN as "artefact-dependent". No change to the registered classes. *Helps Day; costs critics nothing that is true.*
6. **Reproduction gate.** A DAY outcome moves a verdict only if the real-data arm reproduces Day's number at his W, reading and unit within the 3x band; otherwise the outcome is NOT-REPRODUCED. Apply the same gate to CRITIC outcomes. *Symmetric; removes the near-automatic R2 result.*
7. **Add readings R1u (unnormalised counts, exposure variance measured in `realdata`) and unit U-BP (gap-inclusive base pairs, D_struct from the indel length distribution of GAP-07t).** *Helps critics; both are readings of Day's own words and practice, per rule RD.*
8. **Lineage-specific mutational share** lambda_M in [0, 0.5] in the phi_HC bound, with a lineage-specific ALN term. *Helps critics on S2; report the registered bound beside it.*
9. **Label P4 and P5 "informed"**; score BELOW under both questions (bracket failure; not anomalously high); score against K_hi as well as N_hi. *Both sides.*
10. Small: quote Hobolth's second speciation time in place of the inferred Delta; correct the ILS "W-independent" wording and note the log W growth; use 0.46 or 0.40 consistently in the BGS hi edge; drop the tautological half of P7; source TREE from Yoo SI III.14; make S3 operational at the 1-kb bin.

## Verdict
No BLOCKER. The analytic core, the simulation check and the two-epoch and BGS algebra are correct. Before running: amendments 1, 2, 4 and 6 are needed for the table and the scoring rule to mean what the docstring says; 3, 5, 7, 8 and 9 are needed for the two sides to be scored on equal terms; 10 is wording and sourcing.
