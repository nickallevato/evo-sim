## 1. Correctness
**Quotes and classification (probe 1).** The key facts check against the XML. Methods: "the number of human-specific fixed substitutions in coding sequences since divergence with chimpanzees"; "Fixed substitutions were identified by parsimony based on alignments of human (hg19 assembly), chimpanzee (panTro4 assembly) and orangutan (ponAbe2 assembly)". Abstract: "estimate α = 0.135 in human protein-coding sequences, 72% of which is contributed by weakly-adaptive variants"; "α is reduced ≈ 25% by linkage genome-wide". Text: "nearly twice the estimate obtained with the same dataset using the original aMK approach (α = 0.076 ...)"; "the estimates are not directly comparable". Table 1: "Whole-egoism 29925 38135 0.135 (0.096,0.17) 0.097 (0.0,0.21) 0.041 (0.0,0.13)"; VIPs "6249 10309 0.224 (0.17,0.28) 0.098 (0.0,0.24) 0.126 (0.018,0.26)". "Estimated α values represent the mean of the posterior distribution. NS represents the number of nonsynonymous fixations". All match the write-up. Human-branch: yes (human-specific, orangutan outgroup, parsimony). Bias-corrected: yes in the sense of the paper (ABC-MK adds weakly-beneficial alleles and BGS to an aMK that already handles slightly deleterious polymorphism). Neither is a stretch.

**Process.**
- **MINOR-1 (the "post hoc" disclosure misstates what was unknown).** The script docstring says what was not known at registration was "the full-text method, the table (including HPDs for the weak/strong split) and the study's own fixation counts". But the same docstring quotes the method sentences verbatim, states the strong-only HPD (0.0-0.13), the VIP value 0.224, NS = 29,925 and "4,040 point", and pre-computes "~799". So method, table and counts were in hand; only the arithmetic run was pending. P1, P3 and P4 were therefore not blind, and P2 is a restatement of a figure written beside it. The conclusion stands (the numbers are deterministic), but the sentence should read "full text and Table 1 were read before registration; the predictions are checks of arithmetic and classification, not forecasts". Scoring P1-P5 "holds" carries no evidential weight beyond confirming the script prints what the docstring said (as in B9 MINOR-4).
- **NOTE-1.** Pre-reg and raw commits are 21 s apart, consistent with a sub-second stdlib script. No `.host` file; fine for a local run.

**Denominator (probe 2).** The paper's NS = 29,925 is the number the paper's alpha is defined on (alpha is a share of those nonsynonymous fixations), over 17,028 genes (VIPs 4,066 + non-VIPs 12,962 per the Table 1 caption). So for the paper's gene set it is the internally consistent count, and alpha x NS = 4,040 is the paper-consistent adaptive total. The audit's N1-N3 (11,090-24,463) come from other sources and gene sets. Does it matter? For direction, no: every row clears 191 on every denominator, and the audit used the smaller ones as Day-charity. For magnitude, yes: the headline "4.2x" is the most charitable cell, the paper-consistent figure is 15-21x (11x after the 0.75 haircut). Applying a whole-set proportion to a smaller denominator implicitly assumes alpha is uniform over the genes omitted, which is neutral rather than charitable. Possible reconciliation, my own arithmetic and unverified: the earlier GV count of 18,936 on 9,785 genes scales to about 33,000 on 17,028 genes, so the gap may be mostly a gene-count effect, not a conflict. That would make the N1-N3 values lower bounds for the whole genome, not rivals to 29,925. The write-up's "unreconciled" is fair but should add this candidate explanation. Day-side caveat: Blat identity >= 60% reciprocal hits can inflate the fixed-difference count with misaligned paralogs; alpha, as a proportion, is only affected if the artefacts are differentially nonsynonymous.
- **MINOR-2 (report both bases in the verdict text).** State "4.2x on the audit's most charitable denominator; 11-21x on the paper's own", so the 798 is not read as the paper's estimate.

**Strong-only and what A2e needs (probe 3).** Claim A2e's formal statement (claim file) is "rate_human <= rate_LTEE = 1/G_f (per generation), with no scaling". That is a count of fixations per generation, with no effect-size weighting; the LTEE rate being compared is likewise a count of beneficial fixations (of whatever size) per generation. Under the formal statement, the any-effect-size reading is the matching one and the 798 row is the relevant test. The strong-only reading is a reinterpretation of the ceiling as "adaptive change that counts" (the Statement says "what evolution can accomplish", which is a fitness-flavoured phrase; earlier Day review D4 raised it). Day has not stated a size threshold, so a strong-only reading is a Day-side rescue, not a quoted claim. That supports `contradicted` for the formal statement. See MAJOR-1 for scoping.

**Verdict condition (probe 4).** See MAJOR-2.

- **MAJOR-1 (scope of "contradicted").** The addendum proposes `contradicted` "for the coding-only, any-effect-size, per-generation reading" and "not asserted: that the strong-only reading fails". That is the right scope, but it lives in the addendum prose, and the claim file's external field is a bare label. A bare `contradicted` on a load-bearing claim will be read as covering the strong-only reading, where this paper gives a point clearance only (0.041 x 11,090 = 455 = 2.4x) and a lower HPD of 0.0. Also the strong/weak split is the least identified output of ABC-MK (alpha_W upper HPD 0.21, lower 0.0), so the strong-only figure is itself weakly supported. The verdict comment must carry the scope: "contradicted for rate_human <= 1/G_f counted as adaptive fixations of any effect size, coding-only; not decided for strong-effect-only fixations or for fitness-weighted rates". Recommend that exact wording or `contested` for the unscoped claim.
- **MAJOR-2 (milestone 13's condition is not self-executing).** The text reads "It moves to contradicted with a full-text Uricchio, or any other bias-corrected human-branch alpha above about 1%." Three gaps: (a) "a full-text Uricchio" names a source, not a result, so it cannot be a trigger on its own (a full text that showed a pooled or non-bias-corrected estimate would not qualify); (b) "above about 1%" does not say point or interval, nor which of alpha* 0.65-1.75%, nor the denominator, nor charity haircuts; (c) the claim-file comment states a different formulation ("a count > 191 with interval excluding it") and the earlier steelman-critic review said the move waits on GV (C2) "or" a full-text Uricchio, "a flagged lean, not a verdict move, until C2 is extracted". The script closed (a) and (b) by adding four blockers before the run, which is good practice and is the reason this is not a BLOCKER. But those blockers were written by the same author who then scored them, after the abstract value was known, so the addendum's "the condition is met" is a judgement the owner should confirm, not a mechanical trigger. Also note the interval used is a posterior HPD of one model; it covers parameter, not model, uncertainty (the paper tests seven confounders; not re-audited). Reasonable reading: because the nearest non-model route (aMK baseline 0.076, itself corrected for slightly deleterious variants) still gives 843 on N2 (0.076 x 11,090; 4.4x) and Boyko's lower CL gives 599, model uncertainty does not plausibly reach 191 without a 4-fold error across three estimators; say so, as that is the actual support.
- **MINOR-3 (C2 status).** The GV 198 accelerated-genes bound (67-228, straddling 191) is described as "a different test, untouched". Per the write-up it is a subset count and so compatible with a larger genome-wide total, but that reading should be stated in the claim comment so a reader does not see GV as an unresolved contrary datum. If GV is a total estimate for the human branch, then two human-branch sources disagree by 3.5x and the move needs the owner.
- **MINOR-4 (haircut stacking).** 0.75 is applied to the lower HPD even if 0.135 is already net of linkage (acknowledged in the Caveats). Charity compounding is fine if labelled; in the table it is, in the headline "4.2x" it is not. Add "(after a 25% charity haircut)".
- **NOTE-2.** "Whole-egoism" is a source-copy artefact of "Whole genome"; the note is correct.

## 2. Steelman-Day (strongest case that the move is unfair to Day)
- **MINOR-5 (strong-only is a fair reading of "what evolution can accomplish").** Day writes that the LTEE rate is the ceiling on "what evolution can accomplish", and at LTEE selection strengths (percent-scale) fixations are strong by the paper's classification. A weakly beneficial allele with Ns of order 1 fixes at near-neutral pace and moves fitness negligibly. Counting it as a "beneficial fixation" in a rate ceiling compares apples to oranges: the LTEE count is of selected sweeps. On this reading the relevant row is the strong-only one: 455 at the point (2.4x), 0 at the lower HPD, 802-1,003 on N1. The interval does not exclude 191 or 0, so the "lower HPD above flip" criterion fails for his reading, and the move to `contradicted` rests on the any-effect-size choice. This is the strongest Day point and the write-up credits it; it is why MAJOR-1 asks for scope in the label.
- The VIP strong-only lower HPD (112 < 191) is a Day-side datum the write-up lists. Honest limit: it is a subset (6,249 of 29,925 fixations), so comparing it to the genome-wide flip is not like for like; the non-VIP strong component (0.042, upper 0.09) is additional. The Day bullet should say "subset only".
- Model-inference point (D6 earlier) stands: no fixation is observed; the ABC-MK split is fitted. Day's own request for "observed rates" is unmet by every critic-side number.
- Uncertainty beyond the HPD: Ne(human) is small and the paper's own comparator (Galtier, primates the exception) puts primates low in cross-species alpha. Honest limit: 0.096 is still 5.5x alpha*max, so a low-alpha prior does not reach the flip.
- Process: the "post hoc" predictions were not blind (MINOR-1), so nothing in the scoring supports Day's side or the critics' beyond arithmetic; the real test was the earlier abstract mapping and the choice of blockers.

## 3. Steelman-critic (strongest case that the check is too generous to Day)
- **MINOR-6 (the flip survives more than shown).** Every Day-charitable adjustment taken together still leaves 4.2x; the paper-consistent count is 11-21x; the aMK baseline alone (0.076) gives 4.4x on N2; and the bias-corrected alpha is a share of nonsynonymous fixations only, ignoring non-coding adaptation (so a lower bound on the human total, the direction the write-up already notes). The compounded 798 uses the smallest denominator, the lower HPD and an extra haircut; each is a Day-charity, so "contradicted" is a conservative call.
- The strong-only reading is not Day's: his formal statement is a count (see Correctness). A scope clause that attaches "not decided" to the strong-only reading gives Day more than his quoted claim needs. The strong/weak decomposition is also the weakest-identified quantity in the paper (alpha_W lower HPD 0.0, upper 0.21), so a verdict hedge built on it is hedging on noise.
- The VIP subset clears 191 at its lower strong-only count ... only if the non-VIP strong adds: non-VIPs' alpha_S 0.042 x 23,676 = 994 (own count), so genome-wide strong-only at the paper's own count is about 1,227 at the point and well above 191; the lower HPD of 0.0 is a floor on a model posterior, not evidence of absence. On the paper's own denominator even a lower-HPD proportion of 0.0 would require every strong substitution to be absent, which the VIP interval (lower 0.018, i.e. 112 from VIPs alone) already contradicts for the sub-set and so partially for the genome.
- **NOTE-3.** Even in the least favourable-to-critics case in the table the verdict is a count claim against a count threshold; no rescue changes "contradicted" for the formal statement.

## Verdict
Quotes verbatim; the estimate is human-branch and bias-corrected in the paper's sense; arithmetic correct; direction robust on every denominator; the paper's own NS is the consistent base (candidate gene-count reconciliation above). No BLOCKER. Two MAJOR, both about how the label is worded and who decides, not about the numbers. Recommended wording: **A2e external `contradicted` (scoped): for rate_human <= 1/G_f read as a per-generation count of adaptive fixations of any effect size, coding-only, on model-inferred alpha (Uricchio 2019 lower 95% HPD 0.096, 4.2x the flip on the most charitable denominator, 11-21x on the paper's own; Boyko lower CL 599; aMK baseline 4.4x); strong-effect-only and fitness-weighted readings remain `contested` (point 2.4x, HPD includes 0).** Owner to confirm the milestone-13 trigger explicitly (MAJOR-2), correct the post hoc disclosure (MINOR-1), and add the paper-consistent figures and subset caveat (MINOR-2, MINOR-3).

Counts: BLOCKER 0, MAJOR 2, MINOR 6, NOTE 3.
