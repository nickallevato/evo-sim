Overall: L1 gives Day five real results, and the write-up states only some of them prominently.
- His proposed test (Q176) is well designed, and it was runnable.
- A one2one analogue gives a Consortium-like 0.21 on an independent pipeline.
- The gene-set effect is in his direction, and by the audit's own 25% line it is material (+31%, finding 3).
- His arithmetic in ¶15 is correct.
- His single-SNV diagnostic is estimator-free, and it closes the critics' easy explanations as well as his own.

Against that, the post hoc frame-error story attributes a specific bug to an unreleased pipeline on evidence that does not single it out (correctness #1-#2). The "ALL" set is also not the "complete extraction" Day describes, because the multiz alignment is already orthology-filtered at the alignment level (finding 2).
