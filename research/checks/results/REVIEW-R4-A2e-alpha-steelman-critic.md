## Honest concessions (points for Day that stand)
- **Uricchio is abstract only.** Its divergence basis and linkage treatment cannot be checked (correctness m5). In full text, the critic side is Boyko alone.
- **Boyko's 9.3% is a model residual.** The flip survives if about 81-89% of it is artefact (Day review D1). That is unlikely but not excluded by anything in the check.
- **Pooled human-chimp alpha probably overstates human-lineage alpha** (smaller human N_e; GV's own Discussion). The even split leans critic (Day review D2), although no plausible split alone reaches the ~5x needed.
- All the estimates, on both sides, are MK/SFS model inferences, not observed fixation counts.
