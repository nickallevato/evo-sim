## Run integrity
- 714 of 714 cells, R=60 each. 28 cells hit the 1500 s wall guard (all V3/V2, or V4 at kmult 10-30, N_e=1e4, m>=3 mostly). Every censor clock in those cells is >= 9.6e6 generations (> 90 My = 3.6e6), so **p9 and p90 are valid for all 714 cells**; only p900 and the finished-only mean are unreliable for them (those cells have `complete_at_900My` false).
- Replicate uncertainty: with R=60, SE of a proportion is up to 0.065; SE of an exponential mean ~13%. Differences smaller than that are not interpreted.
- Neutral and valley cells at kmult=1 are mostly right-censored at 900 My, so their finished-only means are biased low and are NOT evidence about E[T]; only p9, p90 are used there.

### Hössjer's own spec (kmult=1, neutral or valley intermediates, m>=2)
Of the 100 neutral and valley cells at kmult <= 1, 99 have p9 = 0 (the exception: neutral m=1, N_e=1e4, kmult=1, p9 = 0.017); for m >= 2 the maximum p90 is 0.017. At kmult <= 3 all 150 neutral/valley cells have p9 <= 0.05 (max 0.017). The prediction **holds, strongly**, for the specific target with non-beneficial intermediates: this is the literal content of "far exceeds 9 My" and it is confirmed under his model at both N_e. The neutral E[T] at kmult=1 (chain 6.9e7 generations at m=1, 2.4e9 at m=4) means most replicates are unfinished at 900 My (p900 = 0.48 at m=1, 0.18 at m=2, 0 at m=3,4 for N_e=1e4).

### Where it fails (assumptions the prediction rests on, not its arithmetic)
- **Stepping stones that are selected.** S2 at kmult=1: p9 = 0.77 / 0.70 / 0.73 (m=2/3/4, rho=0, N_e=1e4) and 1.00 at N_e=1e5; S3 at N_e=1e5 kmult=1: 0.73 / 0.67 / 0.53. Mean S2 N_e=1e4 m=2: 2.7e5 generations (6.8 My), chain 2.6e5. (S cells run only m>=2; the grid has no S, m=1 rows.)
- **Final-only benefit with m=1** (kmult=1): Fin2 p9 = 0.87 (N_e=1e4), 1.00 (N_e=1e5; mean 1.9e4 generations, chain 1.7e4). Fin3 0.20 / 0.88. With m>=2 at kmult=1, Fin2/Fin3 hold (p9 <= 0.03) but only for "exceeds 9 My": "far exceeds" (10x) fails for Fin2 m=2 N_e=1e5 (p90 0.40) and Fin3 m=2 N_e=1e5 (0.23).
- **Redundancy.** Neutral kmult=100 m=1: p9 = 0.35 (N_e=1e4). Fin2 m=2 N_e=1e4: p9 = 0.05 / 0.12 / 0.33 / 0.68 / 1.00 at kmult 3 / 10 / 15 / 30 / 100. S2 at kmult >= 3: p9 = 1.00 for all m, N_e, rho.
- **Tunneling/the chain.** Where intermediates are deleterious or final-only, the single-mutation chain overstates the time: Fin2 m=2 N_e=1e5 kmult=1 sim 7.0e6 vs chain 3.4e7; m=4 N_e=1e4 Fin2 sim 2.4e7 vs chain 2.6e8 (sim from finished replicates only; biased low where censored).
- The outcome depends on the arbitrary-order fitness convention (fitness depends only on number done) and on "new feature, no standing variation".

## Post hoc disclosures (everything not pre-registered)

Result: despite the bias, 39 of 42 N_e=1e5 S2 cells and 36 of 42 S3 cells fail (p9 >= 0.5); the bias can only understate the failure, so the finding is not an artefact of the amendment.
2. `d15_summarize.py` and every table above were produced after the data were seen; the "holds/fails/far" tallies use the docstring's thresholds, but the cut-offs by cell family, the per-kmult listings, and the rho pair statistics are my choices. The "p9/p90 are valid for censored cells" inference (censor clocks >= 9.6e6) is post hoc.
3. The N_e=1e5 f=10 neutral kmult=100 m=1 cell (mean 1.15e6 vs chain 6.9e5, ratio 1.68, ~5 SE; N_e=1e4 gives 0.99) breaks the claimed N_e-independence of neutral cells. Other N_e=1e5 neutral cells are 1.08-1.31 of chain (a mild positive bias, consistent with the scaling).
4. Cells at kmult=1 neutral/valley for m>=3 have almost no finished replicates; they carry no timing information, only p9 = 0 / p90 = 0.
5. No standing variation, order-independent fitness, per-cell R=60 and the 25 y generation time are modelling choices fixed in advance and unchanged. At 20 y/generation, T9 would be 450,000 generations and p9 slightly higher (`p9_20` is recorded per cell; not tabulated here).

## Proposed verdict (for the reviewers to test)
- **Internal (Hössjer):** holds. His 2021 model reproduces to 0.3% (status note), and under that model with a specific (kmult=1) target and neutral or mildly deleterious intermediates, 99 of 100 such cells give p9 = 0 and the other 0.017.
- **External (the prediction as applied to human/chimp regulatory change): contested.** The prediction is a statement about a family of models; the sweep shows it is true for specific-target, non-beneficial-intermediate cells and false when intermediates are selected or the target is redundant (kmult >= 3 with S2; kmult >= 30 with Fin2; N_e=1e5). The empirical question the sweep cannot answer is which cell the human/chimp case is in; neither side has published that number. Verdict wording belongs to the reviewers and the claim file; I do not integrate it.

## Who this helps
- **Hössjer / the Day side.** The core arithmetic is confirmed independently: with the specific W=6 site and neutral or valley intermediates, none of the 100 relevant cells at kmult <= 1 approaches 9 My (p9 <= 0.017 at both N_e, with and without recombination), and valley intermediates of 1e-2 to 1e-3 essentially never complete in 900 My. Even at kmult 3-10 the neutral and valley cells hold. Requiring several coordinated changes (m>=3) makes the waiting time longer in every family, and the effect of recombination is small. His prediction is also stronger than critics' stepping-stone rebuttals suggest in the final-only (Fin) family when m >= 3 and kmult <= 10.
- **Critics (Durrett and Schmidt, Behe-style rate models aside; the Panda's Thumb line).** The result also confirms their central point: the prediction is conditional. With +1% per step intermediates (S2), the 9 My target is reached in 57-77% of replicates at kmult=1, N_e=1e4 (100% at kmult >= 3) and 100% at N_e=1e5, for a specific site and any m up to 4; one-step final benefit reaches it at m=1 and at kmult >= 30 for m=2. Tunneling makes the single-mutation chain overstate times for Fin cells by about 5-11x. These are the assumption-level failures the pre-registration said were the real content.
- **Both.** The dispute reduces to two empirical inputs neither side has measured: how many equivalent sites a human regulatory change can use, and whether the intermediates were beneficial. A short list for follow-up: which kmult and which fitness class the human/chimp expression differences fall in.
