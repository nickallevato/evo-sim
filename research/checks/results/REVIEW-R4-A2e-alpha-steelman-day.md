## Honest concessions (points against Day that stand)
- **GV's 0.2% is a null that its own authors flag.** The Table 1 "All genes" row has FI = 1.00 at DAF ≥ 60%, after −0.36 at DAF ≥ 1%. The authors call it a possible underestimate. Day's defender cannot treat it as a count of 39 (correctness M1).
- **GV's own accelerated genes have alpha ≈ 0.40** (75-198 genes). If their human-branch Dn averages more than about 2.4-6.3 per gene, that subset alone exceeds 191 (critic review C2). Day's best source may sit on the critics' side.
- **Every critic-side cell stays above 191 under each Day-favourable adjustment taken alone**: Boyko's lower CL (599-982), a 25% human share (516 at N2), or strong-only Uricchio (419-925). Day needs about a 5-fold error in the bias-corrected estimators, not a small one.
- Coding-only counts leave out non-coding adaptation, and that omission can only add to the human count.
