## Honest concessions: points against Day that stand
1. The budget's size is ln R, not a fixed 10%. lam50 rises 16-21x from R = 1.111 to R = 3. "10%" is Haldane's parameter, not a constant of nature.
2. Under the soft arms, a differential of order unity (maxrel 1.1-1.4) sustains rates 10-60x the hard cap. The step from "s_max ≈ 1" to "the rate is capped" needs hard selection, and Day's §3.3.1 does not establish hard selection.
3. Hössjer's 0.0351 is payable under hard selection from R ≈ 2-2.7, so whether human-lineage R and selection mode allow it is an open empirical question. It is not closed by arithmetic.
4. Term 3 at d = 1, R = 2 (0.0658) is not met.

## Verdict support (Day-side view)
- H internal: holds. Haldane's 1/300 is reproduced, and is generous, at his R at K = 1000.
- H external: contested, unchanged; the flip variables are R and hard vs soft.
- H8 internal: Term 3 structure holds; the number holds within R1 at R = e.
- The inference "s_max ≈ 1 caps the rate" is conditional on hard selection. Grant that and H8 external stays contested.
- H5: conditional confirmed.
