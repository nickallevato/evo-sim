## Derivation of the unstated: which method reproduces Day's numbers?
Reproduction rule, fixed before the run: a method reproduces Day if pooled dN/dS is 0.70-0.90 **and** the single-difference nonsyn share is 66-77%. It reproduces Day "partly" if one of the two holds.
| candidate method | pooled | share | stop-free | reproduces? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Correct NG, any gene set (O121, ALL, NON121), any of the 32 filter stacks | 0.20-0.45 | 40-57% | most genes | **no** |
| Mean or median of per-gene ratios | 0.15-0.44 | n/a | n/a | no |
| No site normalisation (raw Nd/Sd) on a correct frame | 0.68-0.91 | 40-46% | most | partly (pooled only) |
| Jukes-Cantor vs uncorrected p (multiple hits) | changes the ratio by < 0.01 | | | no (Day's single-SNV check, which excludes multi-hit codons, also rules it out) |
| Whole-gene frame or strand error on every gene (F1, F2, RCa) | 1.04-1.23 | 78-80% | 5-7% | no (near) |
| Strand error on minus-strand genes only (RCm, Rm) | 0.48-0.49 | 60-61% | ~52% | no |
| Exon-phase errors (E1-E3, post hoc) | 0.36-0.49 | 53-61% | 21-59% | no |
| **Frame/strand error on ~80% of genes (post hoc mixture)** | 0.80 (fitted) | **72%** (not fitted) | ~21-25% | **reproduces pooled and share** (the share is an independent match); does not reproduce "10 genes" |
| Paralog or non-orthologous projection in a whole-genome extraction | not testable on a per-gene codon alignment | | | open (L1b, T2T) |

Reading under rule C. The most charitable reading is Day's own: the 0.80 is a real property of the full gene set, so the 0.23 is a curation artefact. On this pipeline that reading fails, for two reasons. First, all genes give 0.27, and the non-one2one genes would need a pN/pS of 2.4 to lift the total to 0.80. Second, Day's own diagnostic, a 71.6% nonsynonymous share among single-SNV codons, sits outside the 40-57% that every correct-frame gene set gives. It sits on the curve traced by codons read out of frame. The audit's inference is that Day's extraction misreads the codon frame or strand for most genes. That would also fit his "10 remaining genes" after a stop-codon filter, since misframed sequence is full of stops, but no tested mixture leaves so few. This is evidence for a class of error, not a finding about Day's code, which is unreleased.

## Interpretation
- The Consortium's 0.23 is reproduced in kind on an independent pipeline: 0.207 on Ensembl one2one genes and 0.200 with CSAC-like quality filters. Its fidelity against the 2005 paper is still unchecked (the paper was not read).
- **Gene-set effect: real but small.** All genes give 0.271, against 0.207 for one2one genes (+31%), and the non-one2one genes run at 0.45. Day is right that the curated set gives a lower number than all genes. The size of the effect is about 0.06, not 0.57.
- Day's filter-insensitivity reproduces in kind (0.23-0.28 across all 32 stacks), but at 0.25, not 0.65.
- Q177's step from dN/dS to "natural selection is not the primary mechanism of adaptive evolution" is not tested here. It is a rule-N question for claim DN4: dN/dS averages purifying constraint over all nonsynonymous changes and says nothing directly about the adaptive fraction alpha (A2e-alpha).

## Who this helps
- **Day.** Q176 is a good, discriminating design, and running it here was possible because he proposed it. His statement that the Consortium-like list can be reconstructed from Ensembl orthology holds: one2one gives 0.21. A real gene-set effect exists in his direction: excluding non-one2one genes lowers the genome-wide figure by about a quarter. His single-SNV-codon diagnostic is the right one, and his reporting of it made this inference possible.
- **Critics.** The 0.23-type value is reproduced, and all genes give 0.27, not 0.80. The 0.80 is not a curation artefact on this pipeline. Day's own single-SNV share (71.6%) is incompatible with a correct-frame computation on human-chimp data and matches out-of-frame reading.
- **Both.** Day's T2T/GENCODE v46 per-gene table (promised, Q176) would settle which pipeline step differs. The audit's L1b T2T rerun is queued.

## Caveats for the reviewer
1. Not T2T and not GENCODE v46. Genes in newly assembled T2T segmental duplications are absent here, and a whole-genome extraction can project paralogs. That is the one Day-side route not tested (L1b).
2. Ensembl one2one (current release) is not the 2005 list, and 16,766 is not 13,454.
3. Day's filter thresholds are not stated. The audit's thresholds (5% gaps, 5 differences, 300 bp, dN/dS <= 2) are its own.
4. NG convention: changes to stop codons count as nonsynonymous sites (N = 3 - S). Excluding them would lower N/S slightly.
5. The mixture analysis is post hoc, and its f is fitted to 0.80. Only the share (72% vs 71.6%) is an out-of-sample match.
