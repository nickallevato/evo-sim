# Correctness review of R4 check E (E, E3: founder-event hazard; E4: relictation)

Reviewer: Sonnet 5.5 (correctness only). Date: 2026-10-08. No existing repo file was edited and nothing was committed. Scratch scripts are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo.

Reviewed: `research/checks/e_founder_hazard.py`, `research/checks/e4_relictation_chain.py`, `research/checks/results/R4-E.md`, the raw files `results/raw/e_founder_hazard{,_supp}.{out,time}`, `e_founder_hazard.json` and `e4_relictation_chain.{out,time,json}`, and claims `E`, `E3` and `E4`. Severity: BLOCKER = result cannot stand. MAJOR = a stated conclusion or verdict overreaches or is wrong and must change before adoption. MINOR = wording, bookkeeping or a small numeric slip.

## Summary
No BLOCKERs and no code bugs. Both chains are built correctly and every number I re-derived reproduced. The two MAJORs concern conclusion strength, not arithmetic.
1. The "population-level reversal" (0.0044%, 600x) is one point on a steep threshold curve.
2. The E3 "holds approximately" verdict rests on a post-hoc implementation hypothesis. The claim file's own pre-registered falsifier fired under the literal model.

## Checks with no finding (verified correct)
- **P(k|j)** (random pairing) matches subset enumeration at N = 5 to 1.3e-15.
- **Exact founder chain.** Both modes match brute-force path enumeration on tiny cases to 1e-15. The `real` mode also matches the independent "no-hit kernel" (state = number of hets) to all printed digits.
- **Selection formulas.** `real`: x = (h·w_het/2 + k·w_aa)/W. `hwe`: x = (q²(1−s_hom) + pq(1−s_het))/w̄. Both are correct.
- **Counting windows.** Gens 0..G (G+1 inspections) and gens 1..G (G inspections) are implemented as described, and the 0.03-point difference is correct.
- **Decomposition** (7.5% / 15.9% / 24.9% for 1/2/3 copies; 22.8% under drift; 68% / 32% split) and the incidence arithmetic (q² = 3.24e-6; four-gene split 8.1e-7; Win-memory split 1.02e-6; the memory carriers sum to 1/279, which corroborates their recall) are correct.
- **Per-gene split.** It treats unlinked genes as independent loci and a CMMRD hit as a within-gene homozygote, which is right. The "pooling slip harmless" result (0.92–0.98x) is robust.
- **Jackpot matrix.** Brute-force enumeration at 2N = 8, m = 3 agrees to 6.6e-15. Hypergeometric arguments, with parent A or a, are correct.
- **Absorption time.** The `w[0]/u[0]` form equals Day's Doob-transform formulation ((I−Q)g = 1 with Q = P·u(j)/u(i)) to 1e-13 at four (2N, f) pairs.
- **Ne_var.** It is constant in i and equals 1/(2c), c = pj·m(m+1)/(2N(2N−1)). That is a nontrivial confirmation of the matrix.
- **Eigenvalues.** The leading rate equals c exactly. The ratios 1 : 2.00 : 2.72 : 3.17 : 3.41 hold. The imaginary parts of the eigenvalues are at most 1.8e-7 (numerical noise), so `np.real` is safe.
- **Moran time scaling** (1 − 1/(2N)) and the WF values 0.9318 / 0.9523 / 0.9697 are correct.
- **Rounding rule.** Python `round` reproduces Day's table to 0.0004, and half-up misses the 2N = 50, f = 5% cell.
- **Seeds.** `SeedSequence([20261008, cfg])` is used in both scripts. Re-running config (0,) gives exactly 2.6100 ± 0.0713%.
- **Tenaillon and Good quotes** match the text copies, including the antimutator-decline sentences and the IS150 mutator.

## Verdict
The computations are correct and reproducible, and the pre-registration and post-hoc labelling are largely honest: failed predictions P2, P5, P6 and P8 are reported as failures. The numerical claims in R4-E.md can be adopted as they stand.

Two framing items should change first:
- **Finding 1.** The 600x reversal must carry its threshold sensitivity.
- **Finding 2.** The E3 verdict must state that the claim file's pre-registered falsifier fired for the literal model, and that agreement with Day's numbers depends on an inferred implementation.

The `hwe` inference is well supported as the better of two tested implementations. It rests on s_hom visibly mattering in Day's own Table 4 and on a good fit to Tables 2–5, but it is not proven unique. Table 8 and Table 6 are unexplained, and a few Day numbers sit at 3–4 SE (finding 3). Findings 3–18 are wording or bookkeeping fixes.
