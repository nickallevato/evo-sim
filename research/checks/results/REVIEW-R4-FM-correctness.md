| 1 | MAJOR | §Reading "Day-side point" (F0 human residual); §Caveats last bullet; data design (hs1-referenced MAF) | **The reference projection is asymmetric, and the asymmetry predicts the exact human-heavy pattern seen in F0.** Progressive Cactus aligns without a reference, but `hal2maf --refGenome hs1` projects onto CHM13 coordinates. Take a human-specific duplication: two human copies, one ancestral copy in each ape. Both human copies are reference positions. At each one, the ape rows can be the single ape copy, which is orthologous to only one of them. At the other copy, paralog-versus-ortholog differences with c = g = o are counted as **H**. The dup flag catches this only if the second hs1 copy is listed in the same block. `--filterGapCausingDupes` removes some duplicate rows (its exact rule is to be confirmed in the Cactus docs, not from memory), so dup = 0 does not mean single-copy. A chimp-specific duplication falls at one human position with two chimp rows, so it is flagged. The direction of this artefact (reference lineage inflated, in duplicated sequence, F0 only, vanishing in F1) is exactly the observed pattern: autosomal F0 φ_res H/C 16.2/8.2 at 100 kb, 34.3/18.8 at 1 Mb; F1 4.48/4.08. A further sign: H lag-1 is lower than C's in F0 (0.47 vs 0.59 at 100 kb; 0.58 vs 0.67 at 1 Mb) but matches in F1 (0.60 vs 0.65). That points to isolated high windows rather than contiguous blocks. The write-up says it "does not separate" artefact from real SD evolution. That is honest, but the design can separate them, and the only Day-favourable autosomal cells depend on the answer. | Pre-register a **mirror test**: export a chimp-referenced MAF from the 8-way HAL (`hal2maf --refGenome GCA_028858775.2`, on workhorse; or a published chimp-referenced MAF if the Cactus site has one) and re-run F0/F1 unchanged. Artefact prediction: the C/H residual ratio in F0 flips to about 2. Real-human-SD prediction: H stays heavier. Cheaper first step: rank the F0 100-kb windows by (H − C) standardised residual and intersect the top 1% with the hs1 SD annotation (UCSC hs1 SD track, fetched as data) and with human-specific SD lists. ## Verdict support (correctness view)
The code is sound and was frozen before the data were analysed. The P1-P9 scores are honest except for the P1 audit wording (finding 4). The autosomal discriminating results (P3, P4 φ_HC, P5 at the registered cell, P7, P8) stand on the raw output. Two things are missing. The two places the human lineage is heavier (autosomal F0, and chrX F1, which crosses Day's threshold) are both where an hs1-referenced projection is expected to inflate the reference lineage, and no test of that has been run (findings 1-2). The write-up should report chrX F0/F1 and pre-register the mirror test before it calls the F0 signal either "real" or an artefact. On that basis: FM3 holds as a description (uniform-rate φ, W-dependent); FM4's clustering is supported and shared; FM8b is contradicted for SNV divergence on alignable sequence; and the mesa route to FM10 is contradicted for autosomal single-copy sequence, with the duplicated-sequence and chrX cells open pending finding 1.
