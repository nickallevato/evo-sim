## What this means for B3 (proposed, for integration)
- **The N/N_e leg** (19-46, 15.2-30.3, 9.1-30.3, 800,000) has been withdrawn by its author (R4-B3g), a public self-correction. Error-level by R1 as a change, no longer in force.
- **0.743** stays as B3c left it: a window artefact whose mechanism was falsified (R4-B3b-C1).
- **32.3, internal: `holds`.** No harvested text gives its derivation, but every input of the reconstruction is Day's own: 205 M (Q102), Bergeron's mammalian mean (named in the sentence), T = 252,000 (MITTENS), L = 3.1e9. It gives 32.93, 1.95% off, inside rounding. By R4-X1 §0 the statement is scored on the author's basis, so the unit question is external (N1), as in A3a for the same 205 M. N2d was considered and not used: Day's stated conclusion is "not k = μ", and that survives his own SNV floor (17.5 M gives 2.81 at Bergeron mu before the ancestral term) (post hoc).
- **32.3, external: `contradicted`** as a per-site k/μ. The 205 M is SDR base pairs (Day: "~187 Mb of structurally divergent sequence", Q102) plus 17.5 M SNVs, divided by a per-site point-mutation rate. With the measured event count (GAP-07b) it is 3.38 (Bergeron mu) or 2.25 (human mu). With base pairs counted like for like (de novo SV bp expected under k = μ, gaps.md) it is 0.39-0.56 (post hoc; gaps.md caveat: SDRs are mostly centromeric or satellite, so neither bp route is clean). After the ancestral term at the mandated N_anc the residual is 0.87-1.10 (human mu) or 1.31-1.65 (Bergeron mu).
- **32.3, fidelity: `misread`** for "the required substitution rate from Yoo et al. (2025)", inherited from A3x1: Yoo has no 187 or 205 Mb figure; Yoo's own 327 Mb SDR per lineage gives 52.5 (55.3 with the SNVs) (post hoc).
- **"Median factor of 25 across 55 vertebrates": fidelity `pending`** (cited: Bergeron 2023, 55 species with modelled per-generation rates). Recomputation proposed below, not run.
- What survives: a k_obs/μ_ped residual of about 0.9-1.1 at human mu and 1.3-1.65 at Day's mammalian mu, set mostly by T and N_anc. That is the calibration gap Keightley 2012 calls "about twofold lower", not an independent measurement that k ≠ μ.

## Who this helps
- **Day side:** 32.3 is Day's own arithmetic on Day's own inputs, carried through correctly; it is not a slip. "Not k = μ" holds at his SNV floor before ancestral polymorphism (1.9-2.8). At his mammalian mu and the audit's mandated N_anc, a residual of 1.3-1.65 remains (2.3 at his implied N_anc 5.7e4, B1d), in the direction he claims and consistent with Keightley's "about twofold". His 55-vertebrate figure has a cited peer-reviewed source the audit has not yet recomputed. He withdrew the N/N_e leg publicly (B3g).
- **Critic side:** 32.3 exists only when SDR base pairs are divided by a point-mutation rate. With events it is 2.25-3.38; with base pairs like for like it is 0.39-0.56. At human mu and the mandated N_anc the residual is 0.87-1.10, and at Langergraber 2012's fossil-independent split (7-8 My, parameters.yaml) it is 0.97-1.04 at N_anc 1.32e5 (post hoc). The count Day attributes to Yoo is not in Yoo (A3x1). keruru (KR-05: "Calibrating against the fossil-dated human–chimpanzee split gives something closer to twice that") and Keightley place the twofold in calibration.
- **Neutral bookkeeping:** Day's in-force values 0.743 and 32.3 point in opposite directions but measure different windows (rule C, P5); not scored.
- **Both:** the residual (about 0.8-1.65 across both mu values, 6.3-8 My, N_anc 1.32e5-1.98e5) is the open question. It depends on generation time (GAP-06), split time (Yoo 5.5-6.3 My vs Langergraber 7-8 My), Yoo's own mu for the N_anc rescaling (B4a, open), and the human-specific Bergeron rate (SI Table 8, unretrieved).

## Limits
- The reconstruction is the audit's, not Day's. An exact 32.3 needs T ≈ 256,900, L ≈ 3.16e9 or a count ≈ 201 M (post hoc); none is sourced. Event counts cannot reach 32.3 at any natural mu (it would need mu ≈ 8e-10): a magnitude argument, not a finding of the grid.
- Event counts include indels (about 2.15 M per lineage) over a point-mutation rate (+11%), and L = 3.1e9 exceeds the 2.81 Gb of aligned columns (-10%). The two offset: SNV-only per aligned site gives 2.23 (human) and 3.36 (Bergeron) before, and 1.09 / 1.63 after the ancestral term at 1.32e5 (post hoc). This is a small Day-favourable correction.
- The ancestral term uses constant N_anc. Structured or changing ancestral populations change 2N_anc/T (B1c, B4a). On Day's premise that SVs are post-divergence (Q100) the term touches only the SNV part of 205 M: 32.93 → 31.9 (1.32e5) or 31.35 (1.98e5) (post hoc).
- The fixed-only count (GAP-07c) is a count comparison only; it is not divided by 1 + 2N_anc/T (C3).
- Bergeron's mammal mean is 1.5x below the human pedigree rate; using it is Day's input choice (external, N1), not a slip. 205 M at human mu is still 21.9.
