On a pre-registered grid it reconstructs only from a base-pair total: 205 M = 17.5 M SNVs plus 187.5 M structural-variant bases (A3a, A3x), divided by a per-site point-mutation rate. ## Who this helps
- **Day side:** a real residual remains. After measured counts and ancestral polymorphism, the divergence-implied rate sits 0.9-2.1x above the pedigree rate (up to 2.4x at 29 y generations). That is the direction Day claims, and Keightley's "about twofold lower" says the same. The 32.3 is reproducible from inputs Day uses elsewhere (205 M, Bergeron), so it is not an invented number. The unit question also sits upstream in A3x, where it is already scored. Day's "k = mu falsified directly" has a measurable core of about 2x.
- **Critic side:** 32.3 is reachable only by dividing a base-pair total, mostly structural-variant bases, by a per-site point-mutation rate. With event counts the ratio is 2-3.4 before, and about 1-2 after, ancestral coalescence (Hancock and Nesslig20's "order 1-3" holds). Day's in-force values point in opposite directions (0.743 vs 32.3). The "median factor 25" has no source.
- **Both:** the residual 1-2.4 is the open question. It depends on generation time (GAP-06), Yoo's own mu for the N_anc rescaling (B4a, open), and whether pedigree mu is per-generation comparable across 6 My. Neither side has sourced those.

## Limits
- The reconstruction is the audit's, not Day's. Day may have a different derivation. P2 shows only that no natural event- or SNV-based input set reaches 32.3 on this grid.
- Event counts include indels (about 2.15 M per lineage) divided by a point-mutation rate. SNV-only per lineage (18.9 M) gives a similar 2.0 at human mu.
- The ancestral term uses constant N_anc. Structured or changing ancestral populations change 2N_anc/T (B1c, B4a).
- The fixed-only count (GAP-07c) assumes the chimp side mirrors the human side (as GAP-07c states).
