## What this does and does not settle
- **It settles that these identities hold as mathematics:**
  - k = μ, and P_fix = 1/(2N) using the census count;
  - Day's empty-start formula, his 4N_e timescale and his −π² exponent;
  - Haldane's 300 = 30/0.1.

  On contested nodes this matters as follows:
  - **B3a / B7:** P_fix = 1/(2N) and not 1/(2N_e) is exact in an exchangeable low-N_e model (A3). That is the critics' position, and Day's own since 2026-08-27 (B3g, Z23188201).
  - **B1 / B1c:** both the empty-start formula μ(T − E[τ]) and the full-start μT are exact identities (A4). The dispute is which start applies to the human–chimp ancestor, which is external and not decided here.
  - **B2a:** the exponent is Day's, and it holds at the level of the transform.
  - **H:** D is an input that depends on p₀ and dominance, not a constant. Haldane's D = 30 corresponds to p₀ ≈ 3e-7 (semidominant); a single new copy at N_e = 10⁴ gives D ≈ 20 (198 generations at 10% intensity). The Haldane 1957 primary is still not retrieved, so the definition used here is the textbook one and is flagged as such.
- **It does not settle:**
  - throughput vs latency (F1);
  - the B2a prefactor;
  - any demographic transient beyond the stationary and empty cases (the B1b telescoping 1 ∓ 4ΔN/T was not re-derived here);
  - any claim about which parameters apply to humans.

## Who this helps
- **Day:**
  - His closed-form arithmetic is almost all correct as printed (30 of 33 rows hold, and one more holds on its unrounded input).
  - His empty-start formula U(T − 4N_e) is an exact identity for the boundary case it describes (the asymptotic deficit is μ·E[τ], where E[τ] is 3.73N–4.0N exactly).
  - His −π²N_e/G exponent follows from the arcsine transform.
  - His "4N_e generations for a neutral allele" (Q45) is the exact conditional mean to within 0.01% at N = 10⁴.
  - Haldane's 300 is Haldane's own arithmetic. And Day is right that N_e sets the timescale: the conditional fixation time follows M_e, not M (A3).
- **The critics:**
  - k = μ and P_fix = 1/(2N) are exact; neither depends on N_e (A1–A3).
  - The full-pipeline start gives exactly μ per generation (A4).
  - Day's Q03 table value (125) and Q19 ("8" for 9.13) do not reconcile with his own inputs.
  - Haldane's D is not a constant: it moves with the starting frequency and dominance (from 7 to over 1,000 across the cases in D2).
  - The Kimura diffusion value used by both sides is accurate to 0.04% at Day's F3 inputs, so neither side's sweep-probability argument rests on an approximation error.
