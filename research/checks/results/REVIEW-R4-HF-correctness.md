## The flagged burn-in artefact (asked)
- **Diagnosis confirmed quantitatively.** A pipeline-fill model fits the data. Assume intros start at t = 0 and residence time is roughly exponential with mean m. Then k/lam over [5,000, 20,000] is 1 - (m/15,000)(e^{-5000/m} - e^{-20000/m}), and q4/q1 is the ratio of 1 - e^{-t/m} at the quarter midpoints. softJ R = 1.05 (m ≈ 5,500-7,500) gives 0.83-0.86 and 1.35-1.48, against 0.79-0.84 and 1.31-1.50 observed. Weak softJ R = 1.111 (m ≈ 15,000) gives 0.55 and 1.9, against 0.46-0.55 and 1.85-2.04 observed. Both ratios are flat in lam, as a fill lag predicts and a capacity limit does not.
- **CA1 and the H/H8 verdicts are not affected.** Even in the "failing" cells, the realised rate in the window is ≥ 0.46 lam. That is ≥ 0.016 per generation at lam = 0.0351 (≥ 4.8x 1/300) and ≥ 0.18 at lam = 0.4. At R ≥ 1.111, s = 0.01 (CA1's scope), every cell keeps k/lam ≥ 0.89.
- **DA1 is not affected.** It fails on a cell that passes the strict rule (finding 1c).
- **CA2 is not driven by burn-in.** At h = 0.1, R = 1.111, lam 0.0111, τ ≈ nopen/lam ≈ 5,100. A pure fill lag at that τ would give k/lam ≈ 0.99. The observed 0.79-0.92 with N/K < 1 is a real near-threshold slowdown (finding 1b).
- **What the artefact does decide:** PA1 at R = 1.05, PA5 (softJ part), K = 4000 softJ at R = 1.111, and with max_open (finding 2), DA2/PA4. These are audit or Day-side numeric predictions, and none of them moves H, H8 or H5.
- **A rerun is not required before verdicts.** It is recommended as a post hoc follow-up, pre-registered in its own commit:
  - (i) **long burn:** softJ s = 0.01 at R ∈ {1.05, 1.111} and K = 4000 (R = 1.111); softJ s = 0.002 at R ∈ {1.111, 2}; lam ∈ {0.0351, 0.1, 0.2, 0.4}, 4 reps, gens 80,000, burn 40,000, max_open 20,000; record nopen every 1,000 generations. Prediction: k/lam ≥ 0.95 and q4/q1 ≤ 1.1 wherever lam × τ < max_open.
  - (ii) **mix boundary:** h = 0.1, R = 1.111, lam ∈ {0.0074, 0.0094, 0.0111, 0.013, 0.0157}; h = 0.3 at R ∈ {1.111, 2} on a geometric grid inside the brackets; 8 reps, 40,000 gens. Score on extinction AND k/lam ≥ 0.9, with the q4/q1 rule applied only when nopen_q1 ≥ 50.
  - Cost: about 60-100 CPU-hours (main-stage cells × 4 for the length), i.e. one night on na-workhorse.
