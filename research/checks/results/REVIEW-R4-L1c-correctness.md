## Rulings asked for (task point 3)

### 3(a) Reading A: `supported` as registered, `contested` as amended. Which is the result of record?

**The amended `contested` is the result of record; the registered result is shown beside it. The write-up's handling is right in substance.** Grounds:

1. The docstring's own precedence, committed in 0e0e840 before any L1b or L1c data: "Where the two disagree the amended result is the one proposed for integration, and the registered one is shown with the reason."
2. AGENTS.md rule 5 asks for later changes "in a separate commit labelled \"post hoc\""; 0e0e840 is that commit, and commit order shows it precedes the data.
3. The registered result has no valid arm under it. The only registered arm with a flag is PF2, and the script prints for it: "invalid as registered (BLOCKER-1); drop 0.08130393136958255". So the registered Reading A, as printed, is *invalid*, not `supported`. `supported` is what the registered rule gives when PAF2 is put in PF2's place. That substitution is reasonable (the script does the same for the band: "registered rule, PAF1 in place of PF1") but for the reading it is the write-up's derivation, not a printed result.
4. The amendment answers pre-run review MAJOR-9 ("So Reading A comes out `supported` whatever real pipelines do"), and audit A3 had predicted the PF2 drop in advance. A reading that cannot fail is not a test.

What the write-up must still do (finding 4): say that `supported` is derived, not printed; and record a fact that bears on how much the ruling rests on the realistic / stress split alone. Reviewer, from the gene table: in the 16,545 indel-free genes PAF2 lowers the pooled value by 25.5% (0.2045 to 0.1523), drops 0.484% of codons, and those codons have a nonsynonymous fraction of 0.921 and x = 2.8 (pS 0.0436 against 0.0156). On 85.7% of the genes the stress arm therefore meets all three amended conditions (flag, enrichment, x <= 3). The `contested` does not come from the x discriminator; it comes from classing PAF2 as a stress arm, which the docstring did before the data and after the selftest miniature (disclosed in caveat 4). The claim file should carry both values in its Readings table with that reason.

### 3(b) The verdict-move sentence for DN6 Reading b: `contested` or `contradicted`?

**The docstring licenses `contested`. It does not license `contradicted` for Reading b.** Grounds, from the text:

1. The flag in the move sentence is defined in the registration: "Mechanism-size flag, separately, for P, PF1 and PF2 on ALL and CSACp", with the gloss "CRITIC + mechanism small (PF1 or PF2) = Day's bias exists in direction but is an order of magnitude too small (credit Q180-Q182 Reading A, not B). CRITIC + none = no bias at human-chimp divergence." The sentence's flag therefore includes PF2. PF2 is `small`; its valid counterpart PAF2 is `small` / `moderate`. That is the branch "CRITIC with flag small/moderate -> contested (direction real, size insufficient)".
2. The amendment keeps the sentence ("Verdict proposals as registered, with: ...") and nowhere restricts its flag to realistic arms. Amendment 8 restricts "Reading A", not the move sentence.
3. The amended Reading A rule has two values only, `supported` and `contested` ("`contested` if a realistic arm lowers dN/dS only by removing dS-saturated codons ... or if none lowers it"). `contradicted` is outside its range, and the script prints `contested`. By the precedence sentence quoted in 3(a) the amended reading governs where the texts disagree.
4. The gloss of the other branch, "no bias at human-chimp divergence", is not what was observed: the sign is Day's in every realistic arm.

So all applicable texts give `contested`. The write-up reaches the right value by a weak argument (finding 5): a count of "two of the three routes", and rule RD cited as "the reading most defensible for the speaker". RD chooses between readings of a quote, and its wording is "the reading under which the quoted statement is most defensible (not the reading with the best verdict for the speaker)"; it does not choose between routes through a scoring rule.

## Summary

Counts: **BLOCKER 0, MAJOR 2, MINOR 5, NOTE 5.**

Numbers: every table cell and every quoted statistic checked reproduces from the raw files (179 + 143 + 270 cells by script, the rest by hand), with two small exceptions: the kappa margin (0.02 -> 0.015 at kappa-hat itself) and "pN near 0.58" (-> about 0.60). One statement of fact is wrong: dropped codons in the saturated synthetic cell do not have ordinary pS (x = 9.6 measured, not <= 3).

Band CRITIC on the registered and the amended rule is correct and does not depend on any finding. Hit and miss calls are correct against the registered and amended wording. No arm is broken; no gene was skipped by the cap.

Rulings: 3(a) the amended `contested` is the result of record, the registered value is shown beside it and is, as printed, "invalid as registered", `supported` only by substitution of PAF2. 3(b) the docstring licenses `contested` for DN6 Reading b and does not license `contradicted`.

The two MAJOR findings change no band, reading or verdict proposal. They change what the write-up may say about *why* the realistic arms remove what they remove (finding 1: not on x alone) and about how far the real data constrain the dense-run regime (finding 2: further than the write-up says for indel-free genes, not at all for runs that coincide with indels). Both need a short labelled post hoc run or table before integration.
