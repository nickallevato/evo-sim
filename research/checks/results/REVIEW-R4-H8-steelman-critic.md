## Honest concessions: points against the critics that stand
1. At Haldane's own R and D, his figure is reproduced within finite-size error. The cost of selection is real under hard selection, and no critic should call 1/300 an arithmetic error.
2. At low R (1.05-1.11) hard selection is fragile. Even allowing for K, the sustainable rate is of order 1/300-1/700 at D = 30.
3. The soft-arm result rests on fitness being purely relative. If the environmental change that makes the new allele favourable also lowers absolute viability, as in Haldane's setting, the hard arm applies. The data do not say which regime held in the human lineage.
4. Hössjer's 0.0351 needs R >= 2-2.7 under hard selection. No critic in the corpus has sourced a human-lineage R.

## Verdict support (critic-side view)
- H internal: holds.
- H external: contested. The rate depends on ln R and on hard vs soft selection, and the factor-1.6-2.2 "slower" should not be carried into H.
- H8: the bounded-differential premise holds, but the derivation step Σ s_i <= s_max is contradicted in-model. Under rule N, against the Formal statement, that is a non-sequitur if the step is in the Statement chain. It is not an arithmetic error.
- H5: conditional, R-dependent; external contested.
