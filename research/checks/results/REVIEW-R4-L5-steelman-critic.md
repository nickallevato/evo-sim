## The question

The brief: does the evidence carry the C external `contested` -> `contradicted` (scoped) flip under the as-written reading, and was withholding it over-cautious? My answer is yes to both. The scoped content is not in dispute: the write-up, the combined review (C5) and the docstring all agree that "the zero carries no information on selection with s <= 1e-2, which includes Day's own s = 1e-3". What stands between that content and the field is two reasons (`R4-L5.md` l.258-261). Neither holds up. The first contradicts the write-up's own text. The second lets one reading set the field when its only defensible branch is a reading that rule RD bars from setting it.

## Findings

**K-1 (MAJOR). Reason 1, the weak-test caveat, is not a ground to withhold, and the registered note says so.** `R4-L5.md` l.260 gives "The weak-test caveat, recorded before launch (pre-run note, item 5)" as the first reason the flip is withheld. The same item then says "It does not weaken the low-s result itself, which is checkable arithmetic and is the more secure for it". The fix pass accepted K1 of the combined review on exactly that point (l.415). The pre-run note itself (`R4-L1b-L5-prerun-notes.md` l.29) does not ask for withholding: "That move stays a proposal for the three reviews; the write-up reports the severity limit beside it." Those three reviews are now being held under D-23. "Report beside it" became "withhold because of it" after the result. That is registered wording narrowed after the fact, and the integrated claim file repeats it (claim C l.89: "withheld (the weak-test caveat of the pre-run notes, and reading R-c unresolved ...)"). *Fix:* strike reason 1 as a ground for withholding in `R4-L5.md` §8 and claim C l.89, and keep the severity limit as a sentence beside the move, as the note registered.

**K-2 (MAJOR). R-c sets the field only by borrowing the verdict of R-d, which rule RD bars from setting it.** The Readings table (`R4-L5.md` l.305) gives R-c `contested`: "zero excludes the required rate only if its sweeps had s >= s\*". The reason it sets the field (l.309) is that "under R-c it holds on a condition the source leaves open". That condition, s >= s\*, is R-d, the reading the same table marks "extended ... cannot set the field" (l.306). R-c is not a fourth meaning of the sentence. It is the disjunction of R-a / R-b (s low: `contradicted`, scoped) and R-d (s high: `supported`, extended), left undecided. A disjunction cannot carry a verdict that none of its plausible disjuncts carries. Remove R-d, as RD's last bullet requires ("Extended readings ... cannot set the verdict field"), and what is left of R-c is R-a / R-b, which gives `contradicted` (scoped). Put another way, in the field's comment "contested" means "true if the extended reading is true". That is the back door the extended-reading bullet exists to close. *Fix:* the field takes `contradicted` (scoped), with R-c recorded as a scope whose only supported branch is extended (see K-4 for the form RD prescribes).

**K-3 (MAJOR). R-c is labelled "the literal state of the source"; the literal object is fixations at the divergence rate, which is R-e.** Section 9 (l.293, 297) and the table (l.305) call R-c "the literal state of the source". The source's literal words are "approximately 20 million fixations over the 9 million years since the human-chimpanzee divergence, implying a rate of 2.22 fixations per year or approximately 15,500 fixations per 7,000-year period" (Q275). That is a long-run substitution total. The post never uses "sweep", "selection coefficient", "beneficial" or "adaptive" (counts above). Since 2026-10-07 (ad44c23), four days before L5 was registered, the audit's own formal statement for C has read: "This is a genome-wide, new-mutation substitution count" (claim C l.56). "Sweeps of unstated s" adds a mechanism to that object; it is not what the text literally says. *What I concede:* the post frames its test as bearing on "the theory of evolution by natural selection" (para 3) and concludes that natural selection is "inoperative" (para 7). Day's later A6 passage treats MITTENS's fixations as "every fixation sweep-carried, hitchhikers included" (claim A6 l.6). So a selection-driven reading is plausible and K5's strict condition ("R-c is the audit's construction") is not fully met. But "plausible" is not "literal". The label "literal state" is what lets R-c look like the conservative default, and it should go. *Fix:* relabel R-c "plausible: selection-driven reading from the post's framing; s unstated", and label R-e "literal: the derivation's own object; claim C's formal statement since 2026-10-07".

**K-4 (MAJOR). The R-e row's external verdict, "unchanged", is asserted, not derived. Derived, it is `contradicted` (scoped), and by the A2e precedent the claim is then SCOPED, not reading-dependent.** Under R-e the comparator is the long-run substitution total, and the run computed what the Modern Synthesis expects of that total on Day's statistics: model (i) gives 0 on Z50, Z10 and B10 in every demography and design. Model (ii) gives at most 4.7e-31 (25 y) and 4.75e-18 at 100 times its rate (re-read in `l5_analysis.txt`). The inference sentence is a comparison with "the Modern Synthesis expectation" (Q276). Under R-e that expectation is zero, equal to the observation, so "decisively favors the MITTENS prediction" is contradicted by external evidence, not only a non-sequitur. The internal `non-sequitur` and an external `contradicted` can stand together. Rule RD's own text then governs: "A claim whose formal statement fixes the reading (A2e, a count) is `SCOPED`, not reading-dependent; other scopes are recorded as contested." Claim C's formal statement fixes the comparator as "a genome-wide, new-mutation substitution count", written before L5. Like for like, A2e, a Day claim whose formal statement is a count, got `contradicted` (scoped) on exactly this clause, and the owner accepted the scoping (D-12, D-15). A2e's later return to `pending` (D-21) was about its review tier, not this logic. *Fix:* field `contradicted` (scoped to the formal statement's comparator and to s <= 1e-2). R-c is recorded as "another scope, contested", which is where the clause puts it. R-d `supported` stays recorded as extended. The reading-dependent count does not rise. If the lead holds that C is reading-dependent anyway, K-2 still puts `contradicted` in the field.

**K-6 (MAJOR). Even under R-c, the condition is not "open": evidence already in the repo, including Day's own A6, bears against it, and nothing is cited for it.** Under R-c the external verdict turns on whether the 15,556 could have s >= s\* (0.03 to 0.05 on B10; 0.03 at Day's own 20-year clock). The R-c row cites no evidence for that. Evidence against it already in the record:
- (a) Day's only stated s for a fixation is 1e-3 (Q44).
- (b) Day's own A6 (Z18452504 §4.3(4), 2026-02-02): "If 3,200+ sweeps occurred in the human lineage over 325,000 generations, approximately one sweep completes every 100 generations" and "The human genome does not display these signatures at the required scale." The s >= s\* branch needs 15,556 completed sweeps in 280 to 350 generations: 44 to 56 per generation, 4,400 to 5,600 times A6's rate. By the write-up's own §11 item 7 that means about 28,000 concurrent sweeps, one per 0.13 cM, each with a 2 cM window either side. That is saturation in A6's sense.
- (c) The audit's A6 external comment: "premise supported: classic sweeps rare (Hernandez 2011 trough test)" (claim A6 l.14).
- (d) Model (ii) at Uricchio's alpha 0.135: most substitutions are not adaptive.

"Contested" should mean evidence on both sides. On this condition there is evidence on one side only. *What I concede:* Hernandez's trough test concerns hard sweeps at amino-acid substitutions, and standing-variant sweeps leave weaker troughs. PA-22's "~5%" is marked unverified. A6's own internal verdict is `pending` (scan power). Day can reply that A6 and the aDNA zero both refute an MS that needs those sweeps; but that MS is R-d's, the extended object. *Fix:* the R-c row cites (a) to (d) with verbatim quotes (Hernandez only as already quoted in A6 / PA-22, hard rule 2), and records `contradicted` (scoped) or, at the least, "contested, leaning contradicted, no evidence for the condition".

**K-7 (MINOR). ## Findings against the critics' side (recorded, not hedged)

**K-9 (NOTE). The critics' registered mechanism was half wrong, and the audit's own errors had flattered it.** The critic band's text was "ascertainment drops the swept sites and 7,000 y is too short" (docstring l.111-112). Only the second half held. P4 (standing) and P5 missed in the hiding direction. At s >= 0.05 linked panel SNPs carry hard sweeps, and the swept standing site is on the panel in 8.8% to 24.7% of completions. C1's wider reading ("ascertainment is why Day's test could not see selection-driven completions") is `contested` (l.281). The audit's "5.7" and "4.8 events" were low by about two orders, an error that favoured the critics (l.228). The critics win L5 on window length alone. No published critic made the ascertainment point (claim C, Responses), so this costs the analyst-raised C1, not a named critic.

**K-10 (NOTE). The scope must stay s <= 1e-2. Day's non-zero statistic, and possibly his relaxed one, carry information at s = 0.02.** At s = 0.02 M10 v66 expects 8.6 (3.9 to 10.8) in hard mode and 22.7 (10.5 to 39) in standing mode against Day's observed 3 (l.240). Poisson P(X <= 3) is 0.028 at 8.6, 0.0057 at 10.8 and 0.45 at 3.9; at 10.5 and above it is below 0.008 (post hoc). So the critics' descriptive extension ("descriptively the same at s = 0.02", l.325; "on any of his statistics", l.331) holds only for the zero-reported statistics, not for M10. The blog's relaxed statistic, ">50 percentage points ... 18 increases" (para 5), was not modelled. By the write-up's own log-odds arithmetic, a 50-point rise needs at least 2 ln 3 = 2.20 against 1.5 (s = 0.01, 300 generations) or 1.875 (375). That gives no deterministic power at s <= 0.01, so the registered scope survives this statistic, but it reaches 3.0 at s = 0.02. *Fix:* the critic-side bullets say "zero-reported statistics" wherever they extend to s = 0.02.

## The case for the flip, in one place

1. The scoped content is agreed by every document in the chain, and it rests on arithmetic anyone can check (log-odds 1.5 against 4.4 for B10), corroborated by a direct trajectory bound.
2. The registered note asked that the severity limit be reported *beside* the move, not that the move be withheld (K-1).
3. The comparator's literal object is a substitution total (K-3). On that object the theory's own expectation is zero, so the comparative inference is contradicted (K-4). The audit's formal statement fixed that object before the run, which makes C SCOPED by RD's own A2e clause.
4. R-c's `contested` is R-d's `supported` passed through an unresolved disjunction (K-2). R-c could never have produced a registered move (K-5). Its open condition is closed by evidence in the repo, Day's A6 among it (K-6).
