- **C2 (MAJOR). Issue (b): the CRITIC condition holds, but not on the grounds the write-up prints.** Three points.
  1. Open 1's "about 7 to 16 completions" pools four demographies. The registered condition is per cell. Per cell, 3 F / n_wc is **24 to 184** at s <= 0.01 (25 y; 23 to 184 at s <= 0.02; up to 117 in the 20-y cells). One fully registering sweep would add F / n_wc = 5 to 61 to a cell's E. So Monte Carlo alone cannot show E <= 1 in any cell.
  2. "Sums of exact binomial tail probabilities" (section 4) is not accurate. Only the modern side is exact; the Neolithic side is 32 draws per SNP (`R_MC`), and SNPs with every modern-side probability below 1e-7 are skipped (`live`). A printed 0 means "below those cuts"; a printed 1e-47 is a modern-sample probability such as 0.92^1315 times a coarse Neolithic fraction. They measure how far the nearest simulated sweep is from registering, which is informative, but they are not margins.
  3. The condition is still established, by the sweep-time arithmetic (log-odds shift s/2 per generation: 1.5 at s = 0.01 over 300 generations, 1.9 over 375, against 4.4 for B10 and 6.9 for Z50), which RB confirms directly: from 50% at 7,500 BP, s = 0.01, the highest present frequency in 200,000 runs is 0.956 (const), 0.917 (Gravel), 0.958 (Gazave), 0.856 (Nelson), 0.980 (const, 20 y); none reaches 0.99; mean monomorphy probability in the smallest design (853 chromosomes) is at most 2e-13. From 60% (const, 25 y): highest 0.981, mean 3e-13.
  **Ruling:** CRITIC by the coded rule stands. State it as "no simulated sweep registers at s <= 0.01; the simulation resolves 24 to 184 events per cell at 95%; E <= 1 rests on the sweep-time arithmetic, corroborated by a direct trajectory bound". Remove "more than 15 orders of magnitude" as a margin from Result 1, the robustness paragraph, section 10 and P3a; keep the exponents only with the reading in point 2. Fix: register RB (or better, its expectation form) as a labelled post hoc run.
- **C3 (MAJOR). Issue (f): the stated direction of the V3 miss is not supported, and for the monomorphy statistics the real bias runs the other way.** RV gives a mean non-founder share of 0.0077 (SE 0.0012) against the formula's 0.0083: no miss. The share is heavy-tailed (89.4% of sweeps exactly 0, 2.3% above 0.1, maximum 1.0), so one sweep moves an n = 300 mean by up to 0.0033; the selftest's 0.0185 is not reproduced. What RV does show is that the formula uses the mean share as every sample's frequency: probability that 1,315 chromosomes are monomorphic at r = 1e-4 is **0.90 explicit against 8.5e-5 by the formula** (0.90 against 0.0018 at 853). V3 validated a mean; Z50, Z10, M10 and N100 depend on the distribution. RV is at 2N = 2,000, where a sample of 1,315 is most of the population, so the size of the effect at 2N >= 20,000 is not known; the direction (hitchhiker monomorphy understated, so hitchhiker Z50 / Z10 counts understated, against Day) is. B10 is insensitive (share below 0.1: 0.977 against 1.000).
  Consequences: (i) the band is unaffected (the low-s zeros are trajectory-limited; a perfectly linked SNP does no better than the focal site); (ii) the hard-mode positive control passes on B10 and no longer rests on the doubt in section 1; (iii) withdraw "biases expected counts up, toward Day's band", "the bias can only have lowered it [s\*]", and the section 10 bullet "The V3 bias runs toward his band ... the true crossover may be higher"; (iv) label the Z50 / Z10 columns at s >= 0.05 (hard, and the hitchhiker part of standing) as understated by an unsized factor; the Z50 crossover of 0.05 at 25 y is probably robust (s = 0.03 is trajectory-limited: 0.011 where the focal site supplies everything) but at 20 y hard (0.83, 0.24) it is open. V3 stays scored a miss as registered; the fix pass adds that the registered target was the wrong one.

The sentence that carries the proposed verdict is not in the repo as a numbered verbatim quote.** Rule S1: "A sentence that carries a verdict must be quoted in the Statement; where it is not, the claim file should add it". ## 2. Steelman: Day side

- **D1 (NOTE). The model tests what Day wrote.** Q53 and Q55 both condition on a low start ("from below 50% starting frequency", "from below 10%", "from rare (<10% frequency)"). The script scores exactly those sample statistics; x7 < 50% is a diagnostic column, not a filter on the model, and F counts completions from any start. The gap between a comparator that counts every completion and a statistic that counts only low starts is in Day's argument, not in the audit's model.
- **D2 (MINOR). The write-up never says what the completions from a high start would look like in Day's own table.** At s <= 0.02 every window completion started above 50%; in his data those are "newly reaching 100%" (Q54: "17,806 loci newly reaching 100%"). The json holds N100 under model (iii) and it is not reported. At s = 0.001, hard, v62, the required rate adds 8,421 (const), 16,052 (Gravel), 9,362 (Gazave) to neutral 13,478, 6,510, 14,900: totals 21,900, 22,560, 24,260 against his 17,814, inside V1's own band of 7,000 to 28,000, as is neutral alone at const and Gazave (Gravel's 6,510 is just below it; Nelson with sweeps: 123,376, inflated per C7). So his data neither show nor exclude the required rate at low s on any statistic. Add the row. It also means the model is not unfalsifiable by construction: it says where the completions would be.
- **D3 (NOTE). The rate is spread fairly.** Origins are uniform in time and E is events per window completion times F, the steady-state identity for a long-run average. It is exact at constant size, which is also the Day-side demography; C7 covers the growth cells.
- **D4 (MINOR). The strong-selection result is a finding for Day and is billed as a control.** At s >= 0.05 his blog statistic expects 286 to 3,924 events at the required rate, P(0) below 1e-69; his non-zero statistic M10 expects 32 to 408 at s = 0.03 against his 1 and 3. His zero therefore excludes any rescue of the required rate by strong sweeps. Result 2 should say that in those words, and name the critic claims it bears on, or state that none in the corpus invokes it (K4).
- **D5 (MAJOR). On ascertainment the evidence came out on Day's side, and two sentences in the claim files are now known to be wrong by two orders.** (i) The DAY band's text for C1 is "ascertainment does not hide selection-driven completions". That is what sections 4 and 6 show mechanistically; the band went CRITIC for an unrelated reason (window length). The docstring tied C1's move to the band, so no move is licensed here, but the C1 comment must carry that sentence, and whether C1 external should become `contested` for selection-driven completions is an owner question to log, not to drop. (ii) Claim C l.56 ("Scaling the 15,556 by that fraction gives 5.7 expected panel-site events") and C1 point 1 ("a uniform sample would give 4.8 events") use the uniform share 3.7e-4. In the model the swept site is on the panel in 8.8% to 24.7% of standing completions and 0.5% to 6.5% of hard completions at s <= 0.01: 15,556 standing sweeps put 1,370 to 3,840 swept sites on the panel, not 5.7. C1's own reversal condition (i), "a non-negligible expected count of panel-registered new substitutions", should be checked against the hard-mode figure at integration. Fix: annotate both sentences with the L5 numbers.
- **D6 (MINOR). `contradicted` is the wrong word for what was shown, even scoped.** The run does not contradict Q53 or Q55; every model reproduces the zero. It shows the zero does not discriminate, which is what C internal `non-sequitur` already records for the neutral case. And the DAY band's clauses at s = 0.003 and 0.01 could not be met, so "not DAY" says nothing. If the scoped verdict is recorded under R-a / R-b (C5), word it as "the inference from zero to a refuted required rate fails for s <= 1e-2", and say beside it that the observation stands and that the result rests on arithmetic the registration already contained.

## 3. Steelman: critic side

- **K1 (MINOR). "Weakly tested" is counted as a point for Day when it is a point about the run.** That CRITIC followed from arithmetic makes the conclusion more secure, not less: the arithmetic is checkable and RB checks it. Severity limits what the run added; it does not limit the claim. Section 8 item 1 has this right; the section 10 bullet "the move against him was weakly tested" does not. Reword: "the run added the positive control and the crossover; the low-s result was already arithmetic".
- **K2 (MINOR). The registered scope is narrower than the data.** E is at most 1e-6 at s = 0.02 in every 25-y cell (0.044 at 20 y), and on the paper's own statistics (Q53) Z50 expects 0.0002 and 0.011 at s = 0.03 and Z10 expects 1e-11 to 0.04 at s = 0.05. The scope clause stays "s <= 1e-2" because it is registered; add as a labelled descriptive line that the same holds at s = 0.02 for every statistic and clock, and for the paper's statistics at s = 0.03 (25 y), subject to C3.
- **K3 (MAJOR). Several "Who this helps: Day" bullets rest on the audit's own choices or errors, and the headline one uses a comparator that any SNP panel beats.** "240 to 670 times the uniform share" compares a known polymorphism with a random base pair, most of which are invariant. With c2 = 0.846 (run metadata) the inclusion is 0.846 x 2a(1 - a), so 0.088 to 0.247 only says the swept variants have African heterozygosity 0.10 to 0.29. C1a's sign is about an ascertained panel against an unascertained one (C1a file: "higher than for a random site sample", "exceed the unascertained rate"); against whole-genome data the panel loses 75% to 91% of swept sites; against a random SNP panel nothing was computed. So "the sign Day asserts in C1a ... is what the run shows" (section 6, Result 5, section 10) is not established; what is established is D5 (ii). Fix: replace the C1a credit with the D5 statement; compute the comparator post hoc from the neutral sample if the C1a credit is wanted. Move to "Both (the audit's own errors)": the P4 / P5 misses, "half of the DAY band was unreachable", the V3 bullet (withdrawn by C3), and "the 15,556 is his figure taken at face value" (a generosity, not a result).
- **K4 (NOTE). The strong-selection credit is for a model nobody is recorded as holding.** 15,556 sweeps at s >= 0.05 in 7,000 years is outside the regime of the model that produced the number (C10), and Open 6's arithmetic gives 0.05 to 0.22 for a mainstream model with a strong tail. R-d should be marked an extended reading in the Readings table.
- **K5 (NOTE). The case for writing `contradicted` in the field.** Day's comparator is derived as 20,000,000 / 9,000,000 y, a total substitution count, and his only recorded beneficial s is 1e-3; under both (R-e, R-a) zero is the expectation in every cell, mode and design, and the mainstream model agrees even at 100 times its rate. If C4's quotes show that Day's text fixes the reading to those, R-c is the audit's construction, not a plausible reading, and RD then puts `contradicted` (scoped) in the field. The ruling in C5 turns on that text, which the fix pass must put in the repo.
