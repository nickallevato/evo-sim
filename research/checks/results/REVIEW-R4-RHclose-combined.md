- **MAJOR-1 (C7: "most charitable" is narrower than rule C allows, on two axes).** (a) Inputs. The central set is N_e 1.0e4-2.0e4, all coalescent/genetic estimates. HL l.101-118 rejects exactly these by name ("circular reasoning", "a thermometer calibrated against itself") and adopts the variance N_e. Charity to Day should therefore include his own frame, yet that frame is "reported, not used" because the census is unsourced. The census is easy to source (published Holocene global/European population reconstructions give millions from the Neolithic on), and the check itself shows the tag flips at a census of about 8e5 or more (P2 pre-registers the flip). Part 2 includes HL's variance frame in its central set (case a), so the two parts treat the same author's frame differently; the "text at issue" justification (a comment states no census) is formally consistent but makes the verdict depend on whether someone looked up a number. The write-up's own owner item 2 flags this, but the headline "rhetoric" is stated before it. (b) Referent. Rule C ranges over ambiguous referents. "Genetic drift isn't happening at all" in a thread about the neutral pipeline can be read as "drift is not producing fixations", a disambiguation of the referent, not a replacement of the mechanism (the distinction the B9 review drew). The pre-registration sets this reading aside as "the evidence, not the claim" without testing it. Under it, the literal count claim (no drift-driven fixations from below 50% in 280 generations) is true to a good approximation (expected count about 0, which is also the neutral expectation, X1 row C), so the statement would be dialectic, true as a count, and a non-sequitur as an inference. Both axes lead to a defensible `dialectic` tag. Recommend: state in the verdict comment that rhetoric is the tag on the strict-drift reading with coalescent-range N_e, and either (i) source a Holocene census and run the Day-frame row as a labelled alternative, or (ii) put the fixation reading in the verdict comment as the dialectical core's tag, which the write-up does in part (core stays at C and B2). The practical effect on C7 is small because C keeps its `non-sequitur` either way.
- **MINOR-3 (Day's January data are a weaker rebuttal than presented).** The 0.0015-0.005 per 500 y values are raw Var(Δp), which Day says include structure and migration; they are about 6-20x the neutral expectation p(1-p)·20/(2N_e) at N_e 1e4 (about 2.5e-4). Add the clause "(which he says includes structure and migration)".

## 2. Steelman-Day (strongest case the check is unfair to Day)
- **MAJOR-2 (Q157/Q158: three independent charitable readings each flip the tag, and the pre-registration excluded all three).** The criterion is min F over a set fixed after the numbers were known. Excluded readings, with their values against the 0.30 edge: (i) delivered share D = 0.24-0.26 at HL's own input (<= 0.30); D <= F always, so a literal "most charitable fill measure" is D; the exclusion rests on "Day's flux definition", which is textual (l.272-275, Q152, Table 3) but D is also Day's (Q34, `μL∫F`), and "the pipe never fills" is a statement about the whole pipe contents, not the end-of-window flux of the first cohort. (ii) Takahata 1e5 held for the whole lineage, F = 0.281 (<= 0.30), excluded as "not a lineage value", although (a) (census 1e5, V_k 5) is equally a held-constant value and is in. (iii) The current-size reading S: l.464-466 says "The field simply never turned around to look at the near end ... the pipe was nearly empty" and "the pipe is exactly as long as Kimura said it was", which read naturally as the present end of the pipe at present N_e. The write-up never weighs the phrase "near end". Rule C explicitly ranges over ambiguous referents, and the pre-registration excluded S because it "changes the referent away from" the sentence's "lineage duration", but Q157 also says the two channels shut "on the same window", and B2 holds on exactly that scale. Under S the line restates a true B2 and would be dialectic and true. Plus P6: "nearly empty" is literally true (F = 0.026) on HL's own 2 My window at l.416, which HL calls "the entire lineage"; the write-up treats this as R4-B2's slip, but it is HL's internal ambiguity on the referent, again rule C territory. Day can fairly say "most charitable" was operationalised as "most charitable among the readings that give a high F". The write-up discloses all of this in "Who this helps / Both", which is to its credit; the problem is that the proposed tag is stated as a finding. Recommend: report the tag as "rhetoric on F/6.5 My; dialectic on D, on S, or on 2 My", and let the owner choose, instead of a single `rhetoric` with a 2.25x margin.

## 3. Steelman-critic (strongest case the check is too generous to Day)
