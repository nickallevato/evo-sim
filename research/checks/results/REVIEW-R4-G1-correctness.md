**Simulation code (`wf_run`, `job_*`):**
- The model is a haploid Wright-Fisher with soft selection, parents drawn by fitness with replacement. Multiplicative fitness is w = exp(c ln(1+s)) on segregating loci only (fixed loci cancel). The additive model is w = 1 + s (c + n_fixed), consistent with the stated cumulative-dilution convention.
- Free recombination (independent per-locus parental choice) is a correct uniform crossover. Linked recombination is a Haldane-map crossover process: interval switch probability r = 0.5 (1 - e^-2d), independent per interval, parity by cumulative sum. It is correct, and it correctly drops positions of absorbed loci so that distances remain additive. Clonal uses a single parent.
- Absorption is tested before reproduction at each generation. Fixation or loss times are recorded at detection. The `-1` outcome is asserted away in part C but not in D (not reachable at max_gen = 200,000).
- Kimura haploid u = (1 - e^(-2Ns p0))/(1 - e^(-2Ns)) is right for the sim's variance pq/N, and the observed single-locus controls match it (0.864 vs 0.8647, and 0.02018 vs 0.0198).
- E: re-deriving X from the same child SeedSequence for the two passes is deterministic. The identity sum_j (E[p'_j|X] - p_j) = sum_i wn_i c_i - mean(c) is exact, so E2's count-level shortcut is a valid reproduction of E. Multiplicative expected Delta p = s pq/(1+sp) is exact for this model. The additive expected value uses the sample mean count (a good approximation, confirmed to 0.03%). N_eff = 1/sum wn^2 is the standard effective number of parents.
- The N_eff pre-registration formula N exp(-n (ln1.01)^2/4) is the infinite-population (log-normal) form and it is plausible that finite samples under-sample the tail (N = 1e5 gives 2,612 against 2,053). The md describes this accurately.
- The pair/triple statistics E[K(K-1)]/(L(L-1)p^2) etc. are the right estimators for exchangeable loci. The bootstrap resamples replicates (correct unit).
- Seeds are `SeedSequence([SEED, part, cfg, rep])` and are unique per job. There is no `hash()`. Replicate counts are stated and match the code (E: 400 / 400 / 400 / 87 / 9 / 3).
- Units: generations throughout. Burn-in is not applicable (no equilibrium start).

## 4. Are the conclusions supported at the stated strength?

- **Specific vs any (§3):** yes. p^n prices a pre-specified list; P_any = P(Bin >= n) flips sharply at M = n/p; the horn flip is exact and well characterised. The explicit "external: contested" for m and n_f is the right strength. Add #7-#9.
- **Independence (§4):** supported for free recombination (and clonal dependence is shown). The strength is limited to K ~ 1 (part C) plus marginals in D (#5). The linked prediction miss is a map-tightness artefact (#6).
- **"No cap near 230" (§5):** supported (D, E/E2, consistent with F2). **"Barrier is not the LLN" for the multiplicative model:** supported for expected response; the P_fix consequence at n ~ 1e5 is untested and is probably non-trivial (#4). The write-up already routes this to branch H as "Day-favourable"; making it explicit in the verdict note is the only change needed.
- **Additive scale (§5.4):** supported as a model-dependence result (response dilutes as 1/(1 + s n pbar); D shows 0.82x at L = 230 and 0.54x at L = 1000). The write-up labels it as one of several additive conventions, which is appropriate.
- **Day errors (§7):** #3 (p^n is timing-independent), #4 (per-arising vs per-site p), #5 (non-exhaustive dilemma) and #7 hold. #1 and #2 are over-stated as written (findings #1, #2). #6 holds for absolute variance (s7.10 itself says variance of the count is 250 with mean 500, per the G claim).
- **Critics (§7):** fair. Credit is limited to those who made each point and the neutral-k=mu limitation of the "any" count is stated.
