## 2. What I checked and found correct (OK)

**ViennaRNA usage and structure comparison**
- `RNA.fold(seq)[0]` is the MFE structure at the default 37 °C, dangles = 2, noLP off. ViennaRNA 2.7.2 matches `requirements.txt`.
- The yeast tRNA-Phe sequence folds exactly to the 76-character `TRNA_DB` (verified, length 76).
- `pairs_of` and `dbp` (symmetric difference of pair sets) are right: base-pair distance counts pairs present in one structure only. Pseudoknots do not occur in MFE output, so parsing only `(` and `)` is complete.
- **Shape level:** `shape5` is RNAshapes abstract level 5 (nesting and branching only; single-child stems collapsed, unpaired and loop types ignored). I compared it with `RNA.abstract_shapes(db, 5)` on 1,200 random MFE structures at L = 30, 50, 76, 100 and on all 228 single mutants of the tRNA: 0 mismatches. The tRNA gives `[[][][]]`, as stated. Level 5 also ignores helix length, so a 1-bp helix and a 10-bp helix are the same shape. That is the root cause of the "lost a helix" inflation in E2, and the report's diagnosis is right.
- `neighbors()` returns 3 mutants per site in site order, so `reshape(L, 3)` for per-site neutrality is correct.
- Doubles: the two-site construction `ma[:j] + muts1[b][j] + ma[j+1:]` is right, and the "neither single in class" mask `Md[did] & ~s_in[da] & ~s_in[db_]` is right.

**Neutral network size.** N_NN = 6^bp·4^unp·f is validated by exact enumeration (finding 10). The Wilson interval and the zero-hit upper bounds are right; the walk and the estimator are separate (the walk samples only the seed's component, the estimator counts all components), as stated.

**Neutral sampling.** The Metropolis rule (uniform proposal over 4 bases, reject if the fold changes) is symmetric, so it is uniform on the seed's component in the limit. The posthoc script replays identical RNG streams and genotypes (its Hamming values match my own replication). Stored ν values are consistent with uniform draws to within about ±0.03 (finding 4).

**m → λ.** Matches G1 exactly (finding 5): λ_alt = 0.480, m* = λ_50/λ_alt = 15 to 36.

**DMS.** Counts reproduce: 217 sets (126 median, 91 manual), 206 analysed, 11 skipped (all legitimately: fewer than 15 sites or fewer than 100 singles; I checked the ten largest skips and the loader reads all singles, so no silent drop); 140 non-stability, 114 with coverage ≥ 0.5; 66 stability; 54 manual-cutoff sets in the headline group. The `DMS_score_bin` direction (1 = fit) is correct (D266A = 2.40 → 1). Headline medians reproduce from `d1_summary.txt`. All numbers in the R4 §0 table and §3 tables that I traced match `d1_aggregate.txt` and `d1_summary.txt`.

**GB1.** The file has 149,360 rows and no WT row, so WT = 1 is imposed (149,361 effective). That is consistent with Wu 2016's fitness definition, and the max fitness 8.76 is consistent with it. The report calls it "not checked in the file": it cannot be, because WT is absent. The positions 265/266/267/280 and WT residues V, D, G, V are as stated. Independent recomputation reproduces 5,822 functional, 18 maxima and 98.5% reach at amino-acid level, and 87 maxima and 44.3% reach at SNV level. The SNV adjacency is built from a standard codon table: union over all codons of a, symmetric by construction (the `| ADJ.T` is redundant), degree 5 (W) to 12 (R, S), mean 7.5. It is the most permissive adjacency, so the SNV maxima counts are lower bounds and reach upper bounds with respect to a codon-explicit model, as the report says.

**Post hoc labelling.** `d1_posthoc_refine.py` states its expected range before its run; the SNV, aggregate and summarizer changes are labelled in the code. Post hoc results are labelled in the R4 text wherever they change a verdict (P3 refined λ, P11 SNV).

## 4. Net

The measurements are sound and reproduce. The Day-favourable and critic-favourable readings in the write-up are both supported by the numbers, as claimed, with these amendments: the pre-registered refutation criteria that fired should be named (finding 1); pooled counts should not be read as a supply increase (2); the GB1 margin and the sampler's mixing should be disclosed (3, 4); and the DMS normalisation and scorecard lines should be corrected (5 to 9). None of these flips the headline that exact or near-exact alternatives per change sit well below the G1 flip (λ about 1 to 6, against 7 to 17) while a gene-level "any improving change" count sits at or above it.
