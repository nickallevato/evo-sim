- **MAJOR-1.** The write-up never says that Day's own Zenodo text (s7.1) states the 410 M as "approximately 35 million SNVs, 1,140 interspecific inversions, and approximately 187 megabases of structurally divergent regions, for a total of approximately 410 million". Neither the R1 route (SNV + gap, additive) nor the R3 route (35 M + 2 x SDR) is the sum he printed. This is the single most relevant internal check on 410 M and belongs in the write-up, with the rule applied (R4-X1 R1 materiality: the stated conclusion is 410 M, the stated components give 222 M, a >25% gap, so the printed total is an arithmetic-error candidate on his own earlier text, not a reconstruction credit). Scoring Day's "Zenodo sum" requires it.
- **MAJOR-2.** Prediction hits are overstated. (a) The docstring discloses that R1 = 404 M was computed by hand before the file was written, so "Audit: R1 within 2%" and "Day: some reading gives 410 M +/- 2%" are informed, not predictive; the write-up scores both as plain hits.
