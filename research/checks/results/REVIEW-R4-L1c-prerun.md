# Pre-run design review of R4 L1c (alignment method vs dN/dS)
Date 2026-10-12. Status of the check: pre-registered 536b19b, **not run**. This is a design review made before any result exists, so that flaws can be fixed openly; it does not replace the three post-run reviews. Method: read the docstring and code of `l1c_alignment_method.py`, the helpers it imports (`l1_dnds_genesets.gene_stats/jc/summarise`, `l1b_t2t_dnds.assemble`), claim DN6, the CSAC2005 quotes, the QUEUE L1c entry and the L1 paragraph of RESULTS.md. Hand arithmetic only; nothing was run, no L1b or L1c output exists or was opened. The script was not edited. Same-agent-family review (independence caveat). Every amendment below would be labelled **post hoc (pre-run, no data seen)**. Statements about how named external tools behave are from memory and marked so; verify before they are relied on.

## 1. Correctness
- **BLOCKER-1 (arm P and everything derived from it: PG, PF1, PF2, excluded-codon accounting).** `chimp_ungapped` concatenates the first chimp row of every block with gaps removed and `protein_align` translates that string in frame 0. The frame is lost after (a) any Cactus indel run whose length is not a multiple of 3 and (b) any CDS stretch with no chimp row whose length is not a multiple of 3 (blocks are MAF blocks cut to the CDS, so their lengths are arbitrary). A global protein alignment cannot restore a frame: the downstream chimp residues are out-of-frame text, and aligning them (about -1 per residue) is cheaper than gapping them (open 11 twice plus extension). So in every such gene P compares out-of-frame codons from the break to the end, i.e. P behaves like UT there, while U0 stays in the human frame and is correct. Consequences, all pre-run and all independent of who is right:
  - P is pushed up, not down ("reverse" flag), by an artefact. Order of magnitude: if 2.5% of codons lie downstream of a frame loss, pooled dN in P roughly triples against a baseline of 0.27 x dS. The share is unknown pre-run and A6 does not measure it.
  - PF1 then trims those tails (the selftest shows it does). The codons it removes are correctly paired in U0, with ordinary dN/dS, and the excluded-codon table scores them on the U0 pairing. This **dilutes Day's Q182 observable** (excluded dN/dS >= 2, A5 > 1) with ordinary codons, and it **inflates the excluded share** against the critic prediction (< 2%). Both sides' registered predictions are confounded.
  - The critic predictions "P within +-0.03 of U0" and "P-U0 agreement >= 98%" can fail for this reason alone.
  - It is not what the pipelines named in the docstring do: PAL2NAL / TranslatorX start from an in-frame CDS per species (memory; verify). Fix: amendment 1 (a frame-aware arm PA beside P).
- **MAJOR-1 (unbounded memory in `nw_banded`).** Band half-width is 32 + |n - m|, widened 4x on a band hit; four Python lists per row, about 116 bytes per filled cell. Fine when the chimp CDS is complete (titin, 35,000 aa: about 2.3 M cells, 0.3 GB). With a length difference it is not: 14,500 aa with 1,000 aa missing or extra (tandem-repeat genes such as mucins are the likely case) is about 30 M cells, 3.5 GB in one worker; titin with 5% missing is about 125 M cells, over the host's 14 GB. The docstring estimate (< 2 GB total) assumes |n - m| is small. The workhorse has already rebooted once this week. Fix: amendment 2.
- **MAJOR-2 (bands: not exhaustive, and the order decides overlapping cases).** Non-overlap holds only because of the evaluation order. Holes that return `UNCLASSIFIED` with no registered meaning: (a) U >= 0.65, Uc >= 0.65, HQ >= 0.40 and the mechanism moderate (0.10 <= D1 < 0.25), or large but PF1 > 0.40, or nonsyn fraction < 0.66; (b) U >= 0.65, Uc >= 0.65, 0.40 <= HQ < 0.65, D1 < 0.10; (c) U < 0.40 with Uc >= 0.65; (d) any NaN (empty CSACp or HQ). Holes (a) and (b) are Day-favourable worlds (high everywhere, mechanism partial); leaving them unregistered means they would be interpreted after the data are seen. MIDDLE is keyed on Uc alone, so U = 0.80 with Uc = 0.50 is "neither side's range" although Day's number would have replicated on ALL. Order effect: DAY is tested before NOISE and has no clean-gene condition, so the world "unguided value high because of misregistered genes, PF1 trims the misregistered stretches" (exactly the selftest scenario) is scored DAY, "mechanism shown on real data". That tilt is toward Day; the holes tilt against him. Fix: amendment 3.
- **MAJOR-3 (A5 second clause does not discriminate).** "U0 identity of the genes carrying >= 50% of the excluded codons is below the ALL median" is nearly automatic: excluded codons carry differences, so the genes that hold them have lower identity whether the differences are misalignment or real divergence. The gloss "they are misaligned stretches, not selection signal" is therefore not supported by the criterion. Fix: amendment 4 (synonymous divergence and frameshift adjacency of the excluded codons, which the outputs already contain).
- **MAJOR-4 (blindness window).** U0 must equal L1b V0 (audit A0), and L1b runs first. Once L1b reports, the U part of every band is known, and L1 on hg38 (0.271 ALL, 0.207 one2one) already makes CRITIC the expected band. The new information in L1c is the mechanism flags and the excluded-codon table, which the primary band barely uses. Any pre-run amendment has to be committed before L1b's result is opened, or it is post hoc with data seen. Fix: amendment 9.
- **MINOR-1 (estimator; adequate).** Multiple hits: JC(0.012) = 0.01210, +0.8%, and it largely cancels in the ratio; no issue at 1.2% divergence. Nei-Gojobori versus ML differs here mainly through site counts, which the kappa arms cover: moving N/S from 3.34 (NG) to 2.78 (CSAC) multiplies dN/dS by 1.20, so NG reads about 17% below a transition-aware estimate. The bands are evaluated on kappa = 1 only; for U between about 0.33 and 0.40 the band would flip under kappa-hat. Say so, and report the band under both. F3x4 codon frequencies are not modelled (no ML tool installed); keep the queue item.
- **MINOR-2.** NU has p near 0.75, where `jc` returns NaN; score NU and NM on the p-ratio and the nonsynonymous fraction only.
- **MINOR-3.** The critic prediction "NR r* >= 2 x U0 pS" is arithmetic once U is known: r* = pS (0.80 - U) / 0.20, which is >= 2 pS exactly when U <= 0.40. It is not an independent hit; score it as a restatement.
- **MINOR-4.** Gap cost is 10 + k for a gap of k, not BLAST's 11 + k. Immaterial; correct the "BLAST defaults" wording.
- **MINOR-5.** What the trimming rules do is not tabulated. PF1 (window 5, mean < 1) drops a codon only when about four of five residues are replaced or a gap is in the window. PF2 (window 3, mean < 3) depends on the neighbours: a replacement scoring <= 0 is dropped between two residues of self-score 4 (A, I, L, V, S), but only a -4 replacement between two of self-score 6 (N, D, G, F). PF2 is therefore composition-dependent and it also removes the two flanking codons (their synonymous sites and differences). State this in the write-up.
- **MINOR-6.** `share_of_U0_codons` is computed for ALL only; A0 is stored but not asserted.
- Checked and fine: traceback pointer encoding and boundary rows of `nw_banded`; `syn_sites_k` weights sum to one per position and kappa = 1 reproduces NG; kappa-hat = 2 ts/tv at fourfold sites; pooled-ratio estimand matches the CSAC quote; noise seeds are per transcript.

## 2. Day-side steelman
- **MAJOR-5 (arm P cannot show his mechanism by construction).** With global scoring and gap open 11, a substituted codon is never gapped; where lengths are equal P reproduces the Cactus pairing, and the selftest asserts exactly that. So "P within +-0.03 of U0" says nothing about pipelines with a local or score-threshold step (Q181: "gapped out **or are left unaligned**"). Only PF1 and PF2 carry the mechanism, and both are this audit's own rules, not the rule of any named pipeline. Day would not accept P as "protein-guided", and he could fairly say PF1 was set where it would find nothing. Fix: amendment 1 (local-segment arm and a trimming rule copied from a named tool's documented defaults).
- **MAJOR-6 (the "clean gene" filter is the thing he calls circular).** HQ is nucleotide identity >= 0.98 over compared codons plus no frameshift run. It feeds HIGH-NOT-METHOD and NOISE ("high value confined to misaligned / low-identity genes"). Calling a gene noisy because it is divergent is, at gene level, the move Q186 objects to: conservation used to define the set on which conservation is measured. The non-circular discriminator is synonymous divergence: a misaligned or paralogous stretch has saturated pS, a fast-evolving protein has normal pS and high pN. Fix: amendment 4.
- **MAJOR-7 (his mechanism is only tested where his premise fails).** If U0 is near 0.27, Reading B is scored `contradicted` without the mechanism ever being tried on data that read 0.80. Two cheap arms would test what he actually claims: (i) a synthetic transfer function, true omega in {0.23, 0.5, 0.8, 1.0} at 1.2% divergence with clustered replacements, pushed through every arm; (ii) the P family applied to the extractions that do read high (UT and the noise arms), with UT - P and UT - PF1 reported, since P realigns exactly the bases UT compares position by position. Fix: amendment 6.
- **MAJOR-8 (the CSAC selection as a conservation filter).** CSAC chose genes "for which it was possible to generate high-quality sequence alignments covering virtually the entire coding region" after "removing low-quality sequences and likely alignment artefacts" (quotes-literature.md, CSAC2005). That is alignment quality selecting genes, and L1 already found a gene-set effect in Day's direction (+0.06, +31%, material by R1). The design tests it only through the proxy CSACp, and it has codon-level excluded accounting but no gene-level one. With the real list now held this is directly testable (amendment 5). Recorded against him under the same rule: Q186 itself says the difference is "not a matter of gene selection, quality filtering", so credit from this test goes to the DN3 form of the claim, per rule RD, not to Q186.
- **MINOR-7.** Mechanism flags are absolute drops (large >= 0.25), sized for 0.80 -> 0.23. From a baseline of 0.27 the same proportional effect (71%) is a drop of 0.19, flagged "moderate". Add relative drops.
- **MINOR-8.** Thresholds 0.40 and 0.65 give the critic side +74% room above 0.23 and Day -19% below 0.80. Both are inherited (0.65 is Day's own Q175 bound, 0.40 is L1's), so no change is proposed; state the asymmetry.
- Credit that stands whatever the run shows: a stated, falsifiable mechanism with a named observable; the script measures that observable.

## 3. Critic-side steelman
- **MAJOR-9 (Reading A is decided by construction).** "Supported if any of P/PF1/PF2 has flag >= small with excluded nonsynonymous fraction above U0's": PF2 removes codons because they carry radical replacements, and audit A3 predicts its drop at 0.03-0.15. So Reading A comes out `supported` whatever real pipelines do, on a filter the docstring itself calls adversarial and no pipeline uses. A critic would not accept PF2 as realistic, and credit earned this way is hollow for Day too. Fix: amendment 7.
- **MINOR-9.** "If even PF2 does not carry 0.80 to ~0.23, Day's mechanism cannot, at any plausible threshold" overstates: PF2 is one threshold, and a stricter rule (drop every non-identical residue) gives dN/dS = 0. PF2 is a stress test, not a bound.
- **MINOR-10.** The pre-run fidelity point should stay prominent in the write-up whatever the band: CSAC's 13,454-gene 0.23 came from a BLASTZ genome alignment, with Clustal W only for the 7,043-gene subset, so under the reading "0.23 was produced by protein-guided alignment" the claim misdescribes the source independent of this run.
- **MINOR-11 (arithmetic on Day's own table, derived).** 73.9% nonsynonymous is Nd/Sd = 2.83. On CSAC's site ratio (N/S = 2.78) that is dN/dS = 1.02; on NG sites (3.34) it is 0.85; on Day's implied 3.54 it is 0.80. A nonsynonymous fraction equal to N/(N+S) (2.78/3.78 = 73.5%) is what no constraint at all, or pure misplacement, produces; arm NU tests this. Add the three-way conversion to the report.
- **MINOR-12.** O121 comes from Ensembl's protein-tree orthology, itself a protein-guided pipeline. It cuts both ways: it is a real-world instance of Day's gene-level concern, and a critic can note that U0 on O121 uses none of its alignments.

## Gene set: switch to the real CSAC list?
Recommended: **add** the real list as set `CSAC23` beside `CSACp`; do not replace. Bands stay scored as registered on CSACp and are re-scored on CSAC23, both reported (the PREREG/SOURCED pattern used for FMnull). Reasons: U0 on CSAC's own genes with no protein guidance, against CSAC's published 0.23, is the most direct head-to-head available and can go either way; it makes Q185 partly decidable; it removes the objection that the proxy was built by the audit. Conditions to register first: the mapping rule (NM_ to gene through the GENCODE v35 RefSeq metadata file, HGNC symbol as fallback), the unmapped count reported, and a floor (if fewer than about 85% map, CSACp stays primary and CSAC23 is descriptive). Limit to state: the 2005 selection was made on the panTro1 draft, so membership partly reflects 2004 assembly coverage, not conservation.

## Recommended pre-run amendments
All would be labelled post hoc (pre-run, no data seen) and committed before L1b's result is opened.
1. **Frame-aware protein arm PA, plus a local arm.** PA: chimp protein built from the Cactus codon frame (codons touching a frameshift run or an uncovered stretch become X, frame restored after them), then the same Gotoh alignment and trims. Keep P as registered and report both. Add PL: keep only codons inside locally high-scoring segments, and one trimming rule taken from a named tool's documented defaults. Report the count of genes with a frame loss. *Helps both: Day gets an arm he would recognise and an undiluted Q182 table; critics get predictions not broken by an artefact.*
2. **Cap the DP.** Skip the protein alignment, with a flag and a count, when n x (2B + 1) exceeds a fixed cell budget (for example 2 x 10^7); those genes keep U0 in every P arm or are dropped from all arms alike. *Neutral; protects the host.*
3. **Close the bands.** DAY additionally requires Uc >= 0.65 or a dS-clean set >= 0.65; a high value that PF1 removes from non-clean genes becomes NOISE-FILTERED; holes (a) and (b) become HIGH-PARTIAL-METHOD; MIDDLE also covers U >= 0.65 with Uc in the middle range, with U printed. Report the registered band and the amended band. *Symmetric: removes one pro-Day order effect and the anti-Day holes.*
4. **Non-circular noise discriminator.** For excluded codons and for gene sets, use synonymous divergence: excluded-codon pS within 3x of U0's pS reads as real divergence (Day), above 3x as misalignment (critic); add the share of excluded codons within 10 codons of a frameshift run. Add set HQs (gene pS <= 3x the median, no frameshift run) beside the identity-based HQ. Replaces A5's second clause. *Helps both.*
5. **Real CSAC list** as above, with gene-level excluded accounting (genes in ALL or O121 but not CSAC23: count, pooled dN/dS, nonsynonymous fraction, pS) and dN/dS by chimp-coverage stratum. *Helps Day (his DN3-form point becomes testable) and critics (direct head-to-head with 0.23).*
6. **Synthetic transfer function and UT contrasts** (MAJOR-7). *Helps both: decides Reading B even if U0 is low.*
7. **Reading A scored on realistic arms only** (PA, PG, PL, PF1, the named-tool rule); PF2 reported as a stress test. *Helps critics; makes any credit to Day meaningful.*
8. **Relative-drop flags** beside the absolute ones (large >= 50%, moderate 25-50%, small 10-25%, none < 10%). *Helps Day.*
9. **Freeze and label**: state in the docstring that the U component is fixed by L1b and that the amendments predate it.
10. Small: band under kappa-hat as a sensitivity line; NU and NM on p-ratio; the three-way conversion of Day's rows; correct the "BLAST defaults" and "at any plausible threshold" wording.

## Verdict
Do not launch as registered: BLOCKER-1 confounds the P family and the excluded-codon table, the observable both sides' predictions rest on. U0, UG, UT, the noise arms, the kappa arms and the estimator are sound. Amendments 1-4 and 9 are needed before the run; 5-8 are strongly advised and cheap; 10 is wording.

Gblocks method and defaults (a mirror of the documentation; the two original hosts did not answer). The GENCODE v35 RefSeq metadata file exists with the expected three columns.
- **Corrected:** "a substituted codon is never gapped" (see MAJOR-5).
- **Not verified:** TranslatorX; BLAST default gap costs and X-drop values (NCBI page behind a browser check), so PL38 / PL16 are this audit's parameters; the Gblocks rule against the binary; MACSE, PRANK and codeml remain uninstalled.

### Left open
- No external aligner or ML estimator; the local-segment rule is not a named pipeline's.
- PA uses the human frame projected through Cactus in place of a chimp annotation.
- Transfer-function rates are assumed, not sourced.
- The uniform-noise gate rests on a stated assumption about the noise, and its reference pS comes from L1 (hg38, Ensembl alignments).
- Resources re-estimated from synthetic timings only: run 1-3 CPU-h, under 4 GB, 8 workers; transfer 1-2 CPU-h.
