# Review of R4-HF (steelman, Day's side)

Reviewer role: argue as Vox Day's (and Hössjer's) most capable and honest defender would. Questions asked: does HF test what Day said, does it model the critics' escape routes in a way that would survive in nature, and does the write-up give Day's results the prominence it gives his errors?

Reviewed (2026-10-11):
- `research/checks/results/R4-HF.md` (adbff0a);
- the pre-registration docstring of `hf_followups.py` (8bd9d29), `run()` (modes mix / trunc / softWF);
- `raw/hf_main.jsonl`, `hf_analyse.out`, `hf_ci.out`, `hf_gap05.out` (recomputations as in the correctness review);
- Day, "The Drift Deathmarch" (blog 2026-01-11; raw sha256 192cd61e...);
- `sources/prior-art.md` PA-09, PA-14, PA-15, PA-16; `results/R4-H-C2.md`; `REVIEW-R4-H8-steelman-day.md` (#3 recurs here);
- R4-X1 (R1, S, N, F, C, RH).

Overall: HF gives Day four real results:
- the hard cap reproduces exactly;
- 30% hardness brings it back to within 1-3x;
- quadratic synergy lowers the rate (the critic-side synergy prediction failed);
- at Haldane's R the 1/300 is conservative at 95% at every K (HF-c).

But the two arms that "remove the cap" are built so that they cannot hit a demographic limit, and the truncation arm is an idealisation that Crow & Kimura themselves call empirically open. HF-d also scores one horn of Day's dilemma and leaves the other to a different check.

## Verdict support (Day-side view)
- **H:** internal holds. External contested. The flip variables are R and h. At R = 1.111 under hard selection the 1/300 is conservative at 95% at every K.
- **H8:** s3.3.1 holds as stated. Term 3 as a rate bound is supported for hard and h ≥ 0.3 selection and for quadratic synergy. The in-model counterexamples need purely relative fitness or noise-free truncation, so the external verdict is **contested**, not contradicted.
- **H5:** conditional on h and R; contested.
- **Drift Deathmarch:**
  - The 9-generation horn is internally valid on its premise.
  - The damage input is unverifiable.
  - The dilemma's force now rests on horn 1 (purging load under hard selection, H7), which HF-d does not test.
- **HO-11:** "declines over time" is supported at the GAP-05 upper end (U_d = 3, Ne = 1e4); "quite rapidly" is not.
