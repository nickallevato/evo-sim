B's step to "neutral theory ... cannot rescue the shortfall" needs the *ancestral* pipe to have been nearly empty as well. HL's own H7 says the opposite: ancestral pipes were "short enough to fill", and today's substitutions are their drainage. At the field's ancestral N_e (H9) the fill is 95-100%. Only H8's "best case" input (census 1e5, which is less favourable for filling than 1e4, since a larger N fills more slowly) gives a nearly empty pipe (3%). H7 and H8 therefore pull in opposite directions inside the same paper. HL computes no drainage count to compare with the required differences. **Proposed B internal: non-sequitur (provisional), against H7.** Rule C alternative for reviewers: if Day's "not by accident" (H9) is read as rejecting the field's 1e4 as circular (B4), and H8's census 1e5 is taken as his endorsed ancestral input, then B follows from B2 at F = 0.03, and the dispute moves wholly to the external question of ancestral N_e (B1c, B4a). This audit does not choose between the two readings. It records that on the second reading H7's "short enough to fill" has to be dropped.

## Who this helps
- **Day side:** the conditional definition, the exponent, the human arithmetic (7.8e7 decimal orders) and the X algebra all hold. His 600,000-run WF demonstration is reproduced by the exact chain (6 observed against 9.3 expected). HL itself concedes the steady-state throughput point (l.265-270), so B2a's "latency, not throughput" reply answers a claim HL does not make. At his best-case ancestral input the exact fill is 3%, 12x *below* the linear bound he called "generous", so the audit's own prediction was too favourable to the critics.
- **Critic side:** the asymptotic law understates the exact tail by 19x to 4,850x across T = 2N_e to N_e/4 (a prefactor; immaterial to the human exponent). By HL's own drainage paragraph the observed substitutions come from ancestral pipes that filled, and at the ancestral N_e HL itself cites (1e4) the pipe is 95-100% full over 2-6.5 My. B's "cannot rescue" therefore does not follow from B2 without a computed ancestral fill, which HL does not give. Mansfield's "drift happens in every population" is consistent with HL's own k(T) = μF(T) at small N.
- **Both:** the result turns on one external input, the ancestral N_e (or census) of the hominin lineage. At 1e4 the pipe fills; at a census of 1e5 held static it does not. That is B1c/B4a territory, not internal validity.

## Limits
- The chain uses 2N = 200 and maps T/N_e by diffusion scaling. Discreteness error at T/N_e ≥ 1 is small (P2's mean matches 4N_e within 1%). At T/N_e = 0.25 the discrete chain and the diffusion may differ more; this does not affect any score.
- F(T) is the fill for an allele arising at the window start. The realised count over a window from empty is μ∫F dt, lower still. Pre-window fill (populations before the 2 My window) is not modelled; it can only raise the fill.
- Rule-N scoring of B uses HL plus the 2026-02-04 B quote. Other Day texts on ancestral N_e (B1d, the full-but-short-pipe reply) are not read here; reviewers should check B1d before integration.
