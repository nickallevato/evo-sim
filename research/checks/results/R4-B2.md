## Limits
- The chain uses 2N = 200 and maps T/N_e by diffusion scaling. Discreteness error at T/N_e ≥ 1 is small (P2's mean matches 4N_e within 1%; diffusion F at 1.4 agrees with the chain to 13%). At T/N_e ≤ 0.5 the chain is discreteness-dominated; those values are not used.
- F(T) is the end-of-window flux for an allele arising at the window start; the delivered count over a window from empty is μ∫F (table column). Pre-window fill is not modelled; it can only raise both.
- B1d was read only for the ¶36 full-pipe sentence. Other Day texts on ancestral N_e are with B1c/B1d.
