**Summary: 0 BLOCKER, 2 MAJOR, 9 MINOR, 6 NOTE.**
- The engine is sound. I found no bug in mutation, the monomorphic jump, selection, recombination, sampling, censoring or the summary statistics.
- The valley fitness vector matches Hössjer's Table 16 (s0 = s_m = 1, intermediates 0.9999).
- Every headline number I recomputed matches the write-up: the per-family table, the 470/192/339 totals, the 99/100 and 150-cell statements, P2's four failing cells, the Fin m=1 numbers, and the S2 mean versus chain. Pre-registration order is clean, and the md5 of the run script matches `.host`.
- The two MAJORs are interpretive, and both are about mean times rather than p9/p90:
  - the "tunnelling 5-11x" credit is mostly a censoring artefact (M1);
  - the N_e = 1e5 neutral excess is a real fixation-sojourn effect that the write-up files as a scaling artefact (M2).
- Neither MAJOR changes a p9 or p90 value or the proposed verdict.

## 4. View on the proposed verdict
- **Internal `holds`:** supported numerically for the in-scope cells (m ≥ 2, kmult ≤ 1, neutral or valley: every p9 = 0, p90 ≤ 0.017, at both N_e and both rho, at 20 y and 25 y), provided the rule basis in m9 is written down.
- **External `contested`:** supported by the S cells and the N_e = 1e5 Fin cells, which are robust to M1 and to the f = 3 bias.
- The M1 and M2 corrections change the wording of the credits, not either verdict.
