**Run-time rules not in the registration.** The screening-log header states five operational rules fixed at run
time: R-E2 (pre-transition test: dated before the fertility decline or natural fertility reported, or mean live births
>= 6 with no contraception or induced abortion reported; "unclear" counts as false), R-relay (no values quoted from
other studies), R-abstract (numbers in a fetched abstract satisfy E4/E5), R-E2false-entry, R-derived (Ps = 1/(1+Im),
If = (I - Im) x Ps). The header discloses that "some values were seen before the R-E2 rule was written down". These
rules are run-time choices and the reviews should treat them as such. R-E2 matters to the DISJOINT flag and to PA-3
(post hoc P2 below); it does not matter to the band.

**R-E2 is a departure from the registration, not only an operationalisation** (review 1 MAJOR-2). The registered E2
is "pre-transition: natural fertility **and** pre-modern mortality"; R-E2 states "Pre-reproductive mortality is
deliberately NOT used to judge E2 (it is the numerator of h)". The reason (circularity) has merit, but the mortality
conjunct was dropped. Its direction is upward: h rises with pre-reproductive mortality, and the three eligible records
with the lowest Pd are the route (b) minimum and its neighbours (P2). The 6-birth threshold in clause (ii) has no
cited source, and "unclear counts as false" excluded records in both directions (review 2 SD-5; P2).

- **P6. Route (a) point value.** gamma = -0.028 through the source's formula gives a half-life of 11.3 years
  (printed: 11). At T = 11, h = 0.151 (G = 30) to 0.207 (G = 25). The point estimate is not used by the mapping.
- **P7 (fix pass, post hoc; review 2 SD-1, review 3 MAJOR-1). Neither registered mapping recovers h from the HF mix
  model.** In `hf_followups.py` mode `mix`, parents are drawn uniformly (`pa = rng.integers(0, N, J)`): fecundity is
  genotype-blind, and both channels, density-independent survival `w ** h` and the cull to K weighted by
  `w ** (1 - h)`, are pre-reproductive. (i) *M-b1 on the model*: at N = K with Poisson maternity, Im = R - 1 and
  If/Ps = 1, so M-b1 returns (R - 1)/R at every model h: 0.100 at R = 1.111, 0.333 at R = 1.5, 0.500 at R = 2
  (analytic, approximate). The route (b) statistic tracks R in-model, not h. (ii) *A1 on the model*: whenever survivors
  exceed K the cull returns N to K in one generation at every h, including h = 1 ("hard selection followed by random
  culling to K"); deterministic illustration at R = 1.111, h = 1, log-load 0.05, a 5% downward shock: next generation
  min(K, 1.111 x e^-0.05 x 0.95 K) = K, so beta_gen = 0 and A1 reads h = 0 for the model that defines h = 1. Regulation
  strength and h are separate dials in the model; A1 is the assumption that ties them. (iii) *Scale* (W6): Crow's
  index is a share of phenotypic variance in fitness, mostly non-heritable; the model's h is a share of the
  genotype-dependent log-fitness effect, and no identity ties the two. So L2's intervals are **sourced proxies under
  audit assumptions**, not a sourced h, and DISJOINT is most economically read as the two mappings measuring different
  things (regulation strength vs life-stage share of variance). The registration disclosed both mappings as the
  audit's own ("Step (2) is where the result is decided"); no tool validation was registered. The governing result
  stays as registered; route (b) alone would give U1-MH, [0.284, 0.641], no move.
